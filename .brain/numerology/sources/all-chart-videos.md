This extensive text details a system of Chaldean numerology, using birthdates and names to create several interconnected charts: the Higher Self chart (revealing karmic lessons and potential), the Psychic Self chart (mapping psychological development over time), the Vocation chart (exploring desires and intentions), the Personality chart (showing outward charisma), and the Name chart (analyzing the physical manifestation of life). Meryl Streep's charts serve as a running example throughout, illustrating the process and interpretations.

THE PERSONALITY CHART

We build the personality chart from the consonants in your names. Consonants give the actual “form” to the words, while vowels are more subtle and hidden.

In the Semitic languages (such as Chaldean, Hebrew, and Arabic), words are written using only consonants. Vowels are added to these consonants when reading and speaking. A specific consonant group usually indicates a group of words with a particular meaning, while the vowel sounds added give the conjugation or context of that word. For example, in Arabic, the root meaning “write” has the form k-t-b. From this root, words are formed by filling in the vowels and sometimes adding additional consonants, e.g., كتاب kitāb “book,” كتب kutub “books,” كاتب kātib “writer,” كتّاب kuttāb “writers,” كتب kataba “he wrote,” يكتب yaktubu “he writes,” etc. \[wiki – sematic languages\].

The same applies in a symbolic sense to the relationship between the vocation and the personality chart.

It tells what kind of people you like, your tastes, which facts that you will accept, and what things you will allow into your being.

The personality chart reveals the aspects you share with others and shows the people, situations, or things you don’t like or resonate with.

Used across many charts

The starting time or inception time is written up here at the top of the numeroscope chart, and the energies or vibrations that we are studying are placed along the central line of the numeroscope chart here.

We add the numbers on the central line to create the life expression or digit sum number that we place in the bottom position here. The life expression is the sum total vibration of all the energies placed on the middle line and therefore represents the resulting vibration of the energies that we are studying.

Partitioning of space

To understand the numeroscope at a deeper level, let us define how we humans see and interact with the energy of space.

We partition space into up-down, front-behind, and left-right. This partitioning represents the three spatial dimensions that we have.

Due to the architecture of the human body and how our brain and consciousness work, we humans split the vertical axis into mental/spiritual on top where the head is, the emotional in the middle where the heart is, and the physical at the bottom where we have the legs, and feet.

Now we have two axes left, front-back and left-right. In the numerology charts, we operate more with the front-back concept, where the front represents what is visible and seen and where the back represents the more hidden and inner aspects. This axis can also be called the outer-inner axis. In between the outer and inner, we add the “balance” column.

So we have a kind of coordinate system here where outer to inner goes along the x-axis, and physical to emotional to mental to spiritual goes along the y-axis.

THE AUGMENTATION RULE

There is an interesting rule in a numeroscope called the “augmentation” rule. In some instances, it is not used, while in others, it is used to give a new level of interpretation.

This rule states that if a value on the central line is under 10, we add the day vibration digit sum to the value, and if it is still under 10, we will further add the number 9.

So let us see this in steps:

STEP 1: Is the name under 10? If yes we add the day digit sum

 STEP 2: Is the name still under 10? If yes we further add the number 9

We use the augmentation rule in the psychic self and name charts, but not in the vocation and personality charts.

This interesting rule comes from the idea that the numbers below 10 are very “receptive” to influence and that your day vibration is a strong number that affects all the other numbers around it. Adding the day digit sum to numbers below 10 is the numerological way of showing a strong influence of a number on another number of “lesser strength.”

So lets see an example of this – we see this rule at play right here in Meryl Streeps Name Chart. For example, the first name here Mary – if we simply add up the letter number values we have M = 4 A = 1 R = 2 and Y = 1 giving us 8. 8 is below 10 so we further add the day digit sum which is 4 giving us 12. We then ask ourselves is the result 12 still below 10? No its not – so we stop here. But\! If Meryl Streep was born on a day with digit sum 1, for example the 19th, Then we would add 1 to 8 giving us 9 and then again we would ask, “is 9 below 10?”, yes it is, so then we further add the number 9 giving us the number 18, so in that case the number here would be 18/9.

We also see the augmentation rule at play here in Meryl Streeps psychic self chart. In this position here in the psychic self chart we place the month number which is 6. 6 is below 10 so we add the day digit sum 4 giving us the number 10 here. 10 is not under 10 so we stop the process here.
