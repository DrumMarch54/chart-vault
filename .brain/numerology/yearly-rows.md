## Destiny rhythms
From the birth year's digit sum. Period lengths in years, 27-year cycles:
- Rhythm 1 (years 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8): 1-2-4-8-7-5, starting at the own number.
  Born 1936 (19/1): 1, 2, 4, 8, 7, 5. Born 1943 (17/8): 8, 7, 5, 1, 2, 4.
- Rhythm 2 (years 3, 6): 3-6-3-6-3-6 (or 6-3-6-3-6-3).
- Rhythm 3 (year 9): 9-9-9.
The birth year is the first year of the first period. Each year is labelled by
its position: F0 birth year, F1 first year of a period, F2 middle, F3 last
year, F4 the one-year period (rhythm 1 only). A 2-year period is F1 then F3.

## Year row formulas (dates = when a row starts; "Sum" = the two rows above
added numerologically; "ds" = digit sum; N.age = the age turned that year,
counted for the whole year)
- F0 birth year: Jan 1 year ds · Feb 22 day ds · Apr 15 sum · Jun 6 LE ds ·
  Jul 28 sum · Sep 18 N.age ds · Nov 9 sum.
- F1 first year of a period: Jan 1 year · Jan 29 day · Feb 26 sum · Mar 25 LE ·
  Apr 23 sum · May 21 last period ds · Jun 18 sum · Jul 16 new period ds ·
  Aug 13 sum · Sep 10 pillar ds · Oct 8 sum · Nov 5 N.age · Dec 3 sum.
- F2 middle of a period (the common one): same 7 rows and dates as F0.
- F3 last year of a period: Jan 1 year · Feb 3 day · Mar 8 sum · Apr 10 LE ·
  May 13 sum · Jun 14 this period ds · Jul 18 sum · Aug 21 pillar · Sep 22 sum ·
  Oct 25 N.age · Nov 29 sum.
- F4 one-year period: Jan 1 year · Jan 25 day · Feb 18 sum · Mar 15 LE · Apr 9
  sum · May 3 leaving period (the previous period's number) · May 27 sum ·
  Jun 21 entering period (1) · Jul 16 sum · Aug 10 leaving the 1 period ·
  Sep 3 sum · Sep 27 pillar · Oct 21 sum · Nov 15 N.age · Dec 8 sum.
- Name-change years have their own ~20 variants (old/new LE and pillar
  depending on the month of the change); they are in the slides.
- Columns: main rows (digit sum of the whole year); shadow rows (last two
  digits of the year, 2017 → 17/8) for the inner energy; an aura column
  between them; psychic-self rows use the psyche LE and pillar instead of the
  name chart's. Day vibrations: current date ds, personal day ds, sum, LE ds,
  sum, current month ds, sum, current year ds, sum; a date's shadow is its last
  digit (26 → 6, 3 → 0).
