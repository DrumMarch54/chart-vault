## Hierarchy
- Stand-alone "first link" vibrations (day, month, year, names, LE) are the
  strongest (roughly 60-70%). Auras are created by addition and show the
  resulting behavior, strategy or field (principle of 3). A diamond is the
  structure holding both parents and their aura (principle of 4). The center
  is the immediate output; the diamond is why.
- Top auras use the day: conscious, "made-conscious". Bottom auras use the LE:
  unconscious, emotional reaction, the ego cannot see below the name line.
  Left auras face the public, right auras the intimate world.
- Day = who you are; aura = what you do. Day is conscious, LE unconscious,
  first name extrovert, surname introvert. Day = Shiva, LE = Shakti.
- Start with the Nexus (day + LP) before anything else. Then look at centers
  and life expressions for the overall direction. Prioritize patterns, repeated
  numbers, dichotomies ("on one hand... on the other").
- Collapse and expand: read all the diamonds for the big picture, or just a
  center number for a talking point (an 8 in a center → cynicism/over-control
  whatever diamond made it).
- Imbalance can transmute into propulsion; a pain point can become an asset.
