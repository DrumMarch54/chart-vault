# Combination cards the owner graded (2026-11-26 and 27): the models, word for word

Read `combinations.md` first (the rules and every correction). These are the cards the owner read and passed in
the training chat, with his fixes already applied. They are the MODELS for every card written later, and they go
into the app as they stand. Written before two late rules, so when one is put into the app: add two more phrases
(three in all), add "At Its Worst" where it is missing, and keep everything else unchanged.

Parts, always in this order: The Phrases · By Position · What They Share (or What Sets Them Apart) · What the
Course Says · My Reasoning (separate points) · At Its Best · At Its Worst. Every claim carries its number in
brackets. "They", never "he". The day is the personality, never "the core" (the core is the life path).
