# What each position means (short)

From the website articles (Essence, Psyche and Name Chart Position Meanings;
Higher Self, Psyche, Vocation, Personality, Name Chart website pages) and the
course manual.

## The five charts
- Essence / Higher Self (purple, top): birth date only, never changes. Soul,
  karma, talents, life mission. Most active in childhood and old age.
- Psyche (blue): birth date placed in a numeroscope. How you think and feel,
  development from childhood to adulthood, mental and emotional diamonds.
- Name chart (gold, middle): names + day. The most physical chart: what you
  attract, money, love life, health, how energy flows between you and others.
- Vocation chart (green, bottom right): vowels. Heart's desire, deepest
  desires and hidden motives; a resonator (law of attraction). The word is a
  spin on "vowels", not career.
- Personality chart (red, bottom left): consonants. Charisma, first impression,
  how others see you. Day vibration + big personality number are the best
  career indicators (statistics on 100,000 celebrities).

## Essence chart
- Day vibration: base archetype of the personality, 30-50% of visible traits,
  portal between higher self and human self, the karmic lesson chosen. "The
  eye/I of consciousness", the sun the names orbit.
- Month: relationship with the outer world, conscious mind, early years, "the
  soul's first name". Year: inner world, subconscious, emotions, shared by a
  generation, "the soul's surname", a heavy rucksack.
- Outer aura/diamond (day+month): action code, social strategy, how you meet
  new people and how they first see you.
- Inner aura/diamond (day+year): inner emotional life, private coping
  strategy, closest friends and loved ones.
- Dharma line/diamond (month+year): communication between conscious and
  subconscious, the supramental mind, the "code" for living the life purpose,
  spiritual hat / crown. Not the purpose itself.
- Little life path: core of the soul, karma from former lives, felt at home and
  at night. Big (higher) life path: greatest potential, awakens after 30,
  "guiding star".
- DV-LP diamond (Nexus): ego in conversation with the soul, natural talent,
  the mental filter used to process one's fate. Start every reading here.
- Target number (day + dharma ds): the sweet spot; aiming at it is the fastest
  spiritual leap. Ego + dharma = karma.

## Name chart
- First name: outer, radiant side; first impression; how you deal with the
  world. Middle name: inner processing, "my middle name is...", huge effect on
  LE, both centers and the bottom auras. Surname: inner, receptive side;
  family karma, inner life, closest ones.
- Life expression: all names added; what you attract; focus, dreams, goals;
  acts autonomously like a shadow. Letter count works with it.
- Top left aura (brand/public): how strangers and the media see you; protects
  the heart center. Top right (friends): what you project to close friends.
  Bottom left (associates): colleagues, acquaintances; protects the solar
  plexus. Bottom right (love life): family, partner, children.
- Heart/mind center (top circle): communication, values, thought forms, upper
  body health, cash flow, your brand. Closer to the throat chakra than the
  heart chakra. Sub-vibrations show the middle names' influence.
- Solar plexus/emotion center (bottom circle): inner emotional life, self
  esteem, sex and love life, lower body health, gut. The subconscious engine.
- Spine: inner strength, how your archetype deals with what life brings.
- Pillar: the ground you stand on; strong pillar compensates for weak numbers;
  a weak one topples the chart. Root chakra.
- Chakra overlay: pillar root, LE + solar plexus sacral/solar plexus, name line
  heart, heart center throat, day third eye, higher self crown.

## Psyche chart
- Mental diamond (top auras): what you think about and how thoughts are
  structured. Emotional diamond (bottom auras): deepest needs in love and
  emotion, how emotions from others are processed.
- Lower right aura = childhood, upper right = teenage years (ego structure),
  lower left = twenties (social intelligence), upper left + psychic month +
  day = adult potential (28+). The transition at 27-28 is the psyche challenge
  (psychic month); 12, 13, 16, 18 are the hard ones.
- Psyche pillar: how the psyche finds stability; 11, 12, 16, 18 there bring
  neediness, anxiety or anger. Psyche life expression: the development path
  followed to feel happy; its diamond (psychic month + year) is the inner
  story.
- Polarity: masculine numbers → rational; feminine → intuitive; count which
  numbers repeat ("color of the psyche").

## Vocation and personality charts
- Vocation mind diamond: the heart's intention, the faintest whisper. Vocation
  emotion diamond: desires and longings. Heart's desire numbers (little/big):
  will power, the conclusion of the chart; big = long-term, little = daily.
- Personality numbers (little/big): charisma seen from a distance; big = who
  you aspire to become in others' eyes, little = who you project daily.
  Mental diamond: how others experience your mind. Emotion diamond: felt
  within 2 meters, body language.
- Both charts lie dormant until awakened by conscious intent. Changing a name
  changes both.
