# Psyche charts (Module 7 powerpoint, Psyche website article, owner's notes)

Construction: see `chart-construction.md`. Two charts, inner (IPS) and outer
(OPS). The inner one is the birth numbers "seen with neutral eyes"; the outer
one has the full influence of the day vibration (the outer aura digit sum
replaces the month, lifted by 9 into the teenage range when under 10).

## The "teenage" numbers 10-18 (what the ego does to a pure number)
- 1 acts on desire and what feels right. 10/1 tries to enact all cosmic
  principles and becomes manic, over-perfectionist.
- 2 feels into the other, "let us play". 11/2 always has itself AND the other
  in the picture.
- 3 tackles problems with strategy and follow-through. 12/3 feels vulnerable
  inside, overthinks, panics, feels like a victim.
- 4 builds something solid. 13/4 takes its ego into the building and feels like
  an outsider when left out.
- 5 communicates. 14/5 adds networking, business, money: a salesperson.
- 6 solves with love. 15/6 creates emotional drama: love plus ego.
- 7 sees all perspectives. 16/7 reacts with fear, overthinking, obsession,
  loses grounding.
- 8 tackles head-on. 17/8 is uncomfortable in the spotlight, hesitates.
- 9 acts decisively. 18/9 overreacts: frustration, anger, fanaticism.

## Stages
Childhood: balance, finding one's legs (lower inner aura, year + LE).
Teenage: "I see" (upper inner aura, day + year). Twenties: "I/we see" (lower
outer aura, month + LE). Thirties: "I/we see and conquer" (upper outer aura,
day + month). Psyche challenge number = the psychic month = adult transition
(prefrontal cortex meets the limbic system). Solar plexus = limbic system.
Pillar = brain stem.
