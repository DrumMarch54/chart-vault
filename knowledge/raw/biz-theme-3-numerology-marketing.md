#### HOUSE NUMBER BALANCING

- 1 — Idea Letters → A, I, J, Q, Y

- Why: These letters all have a sharp, initiating feel — they “cut through” and begin things (A starts the alphabet, I stands alone, J jumps, Q is quirky and unique, Y branches out like a fork in the road).

They mirror the 1-energy of originality, initiative, and the spark of a new idea.

- 2 — Balanced Letters → B, K, R

- Why: These letters feel steady and symmetrical: B has two equal loops, K has an inward-outward balance, and R combines softness with a grounded leg. They resonate with the 2-energy of harmony, cooperation, emotional balance, and gentle relational flow.

- 3 — Creative Curve Letters → C, G, L, S

- Why: All these letters have curves, waves or flowing lines, matching 3’s energy of creativity, communication, movement, joy, and artistic playfulness. C curves open; G spirals; L flows down and out; S is a full wave — perfect for expressive 3.

- 4 — Determined Letters → D, M, T

- Why: These letters are solid, structured, and grounded in shape.

D stands firm, M is like a mountain, T is a crossbar stabilizing the vertical line. This matches 4-energy: discipline, order, foundations, and practical mastery.

- 5 — Explorer Letters → E, H, N, X

- Why: These letters visually “move out” in multiple directions.

E reaches outward, H forms a bridge, N steps diagonally, and X expands from a center point. They embody 5-energy: change, travel, exploration, freedom, curiosity, and constant motion.

- 6 — Warm V-Letters → U, V, W

- Why: These letters feel rounded, open, and embracing — even visually shaped like vessels that “hold” warmth or care. They correspond to 6-energy: family, love, harmony, protection, nurturing, and heartfelt presence.

- 7 — Mystic OZ Letters → O, Z

- Why: O is a closed circle (inner world, depth), and Z has a zig-zag “lightning bolt” quality — both carrying mystery and magic. The “Wizard of OZ” association also reinforces 7-energy: intuition, spiritual insight, hidden knowledge, and the search for truth.

- 8 — Power Letters → F, P

- Why: Both letters strike forward with force: F pushes outward in three directions, P has a strong down-stroke and a bold forward loop. They match 8-energy: strength, authority, manifestation, ambition, and material power.
