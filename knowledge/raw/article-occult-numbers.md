# Occult Numbers

topic: numbers

With 78/6, 87/6, 71/8, (63/9) you largely operate in the shadows and have deep insights into other people and their whereabouts.

 I think if the bearer has pure intentions, there can be some element of "ethical trickery" or sleight of hand. You can be something like a veiled strategist with high ideals; occult (hidden) healing. It's very much about "white magic" facilitated from the shadows (the unconscious).

In terms of visible character traits, you give off a mysterious yet enchanting vibe that you know “the secrets” and so you play on that, using it to your advantage. Almost like a private investigator who understands things that you and other people don't; “The man behind the curtain”

 Sometimes a person can subtly shape (manipulate) situations, wording, and framing for the betterment of others.

With these numbers you play your cards...

 Withholding specific information, playing dumb (to some ethical degree), asking questions to invoke a specific response, managing subtleties etc. in order to get to the heart of a matter and invoke a "higher octave".

I was fortunate enough to work with a Jungian psychologist for a couple sessions. He very much embodied this stuff I'm talking about. I can only imagine he has these numbers in his chart (if I could see it).

A healthy occult number works both in the light and the dark.

On the other hand if a person uses these archetypes solely for personal gain, the "secrets" and insights you have about others turn to whispers in the shadows. Trashing them behind their back yet acting like a different person to their face; fakeness and backstabbing. Your “occult” insights are used for blackmail, coercion and manipulation (in a spectrum of degrees, it could just be in slight ways).

Your charisma is a facade of clever acting that feeds your massive calculated ego. Your flavorful personality hides a narcissist with little to no empathy or consideration for others; Psychopathy. (Once again to a range of degrees)
