## 52/7, 61/7, 70/7, 79/7, 88/7 and 97/7

#### 52/7 The Philosophical Adventuror

Philosophical, popular, and curious about experiences and people. In balance, this vibration brings a strong sense of adventure, humor, warmth, and universalism.

In imbalance, life may feel full of problems, overthinking, chaotic upheavals, and it can be the most verbally aggressive number. In balance, it exudes a majestic, focused, philosophical, and strong presence.

#### 61/7 The Ethical Purist

This vibration focuses on consciously letting go of the material world to follow a spiritual path, finding joy in pure being.

In imbalance, there may be too many rigid principles imposed on others against their will and a lack of joy in life.

In balance, it understands the deepest spiritual laws and supports living a free, simple, and detached life. This vibration is rarely used in numeroscopes due to its ”dry” vibe and the challenge of mastering it.

#### 70/7 The Cosmic Seeker

A spiritual, cosmic, intuitive, and introspective vibration. It analyzes problems on all levels of life and finds deeply intuitive solutions. It carries a literary and scientific vibe, accumulating knowledge that transforms into wisdom over time.

In imbalance, there may be chaos, extreme criticism, and a desire to withdraw from the world. In balance, it allows you to let go and trust universal intelligence to do the work.

#### 79/7 The Analytical Sage

79/7 often manifests as either highly spiritual or highly scientific/atheistic. What unites these opposites is the ability to analyze and understand underlying truths.

In imbalance, it can create fearfulness, self-criticism, and a tendency to expect the worst. There may also be judgmental attitudes toward people who don’t share the same beliefs or values.

When balanced, it provides an acute analytical mind that observes phenomena and extracts their essential essence and knowledge.

#### 88/7 Master of Karma

88/7 brings great beauty, charisma, and a magnetic presence. However, it also carries a fearful nature, worrying about the consequences of one’s actions.

This number offers a powerful lesson in learning from mistakes, whether from this life or past ones. It is challenging to master and often comes with many life trials.

In imbalance, it can create paralysis and an inability to act due to inner conflicts and doubt. There’s often a sense of walking a tightrope, where deviations can have unpredictable consequences. In balance, it turns you into a master of analysis, capable of seeing both sides of an issue and navigating through contradictions.

#### 97/7 The Transcendentalist

97/7 represents miracles, transcending reality, and uncovering the essence of existence.

It provides insight into the most existential aspects of life, fearlessly exploring the boundaries of the conscious and unconscious mind, life and death, light and darkness, emptiness, eternity, and truth. This is an incredibly deep and intuitive vibration. In imbalance, there is a risk of using universal knowledge selfishly, as seen in the ”ruthless guru” archetype who exploits followers for personal gain. Before reaching its peak, 97/7 often journeys through a valley of deep existential fear and anxiety. Once this fear is embraced, understood, and overcome, 97/7 becomes an unstoppable force. It can appear as a formidable opponent, having conquered its fear of death.

### 52/7 THE PHILOSOPHICAL ADVENTUROR

#### THE RESTLESS DEBATER

Philosophical, popular and curious about experiences and other people.

In balance, this vibration brings a great sense of adventure, sense of humor, warmth and universality.

In imbalance, life can be full of problems, with endless twists and turns. There are chaotic upheavals and can be the most verbally aggressive number. In balance, it gives a majestic, focused, philosophical and strong appearance - you are a kind of wisdom and philosopher king. Great wanderlust 52/7 loves to travel and visit new places - and they are often unusual places. 52/7 could for example travel to Gambia or Kazakhstan simply to see how people live in these exotic places. 52/7 also loves philosophy and is always ready to discuss any view of life. However, it's not as much of an internal process as at 25/7, for example. This is a very extroverted process that can almost be characterized as a kind of "external entertainment" for the restless mind of the 52/7. Great curiosity The 52/7 energy can be very curious. When you sit across from a 52/7 person, he/she may look at you and try to figure out how you are feeling. They may pry things out of you in all sorts of ways, and the more you resist, the more they try to get inside. There can be a definite "trickster" energy to 52/7, questioning you, teasing you and drilling into you to get behind your surface.

52/7 can be overly curious about their friends. They might pick up

your phone and start looking through it or look through the papers or notepads you have next to you. You can't turn your back and go to the bathroom, for example, without returning to find 52/7 looking through your personal belongings. When you confront 52/7 about it, it will respond with the most natural "I'm just looking" with a twinkle in its eye (which of course makes it difficult to reprimand it). Humor and teasing 52/7 has a tremendous sense of humor, which most of all can be characterized by a kind of childish teasing combined with slapstick comedy. 52/7 can see the funny in interpersonal situations and caricature it with great precision so that everyone laughs and joins in. In their humor lies an intense "teasing" that can often be felt in the stomach, as if 52/7 is always trying to get under your skin and see how you react. Of course, it's best not to get offended and quickly change the subject so you don't get caught up in 52/7's agenda - which often has no other purpose than to see if it can succeed in teasing you or knock you off your feet.

This teasing doesn't have to be malicious. The more a 52/7 feels comfortable with others, the more it teases. But it can also use the teasing strategy to get under people's skin, but not everyone likes to be teased.

Great professionalism The 52/7 has great professionalism where it can quickly familiarize itself with new material.

So behind the wanderlust, curiosity and humor, there is an intelligent mind that actively "collects material" and categorizes it into life experiences. A kind of roadmap for how to navigate through future situations. So you shouldn't discount 52/7's high level of discussion, curiosity and humor. There is a consciousness behind it that is measuring, weighing, categorizing, experiencing and gathering wisdom. We must remember that it is a 7 wisdom energy.

52/7 and 25/7 - two quite different energies Where 25/7 is a rather soft vibration outwardly, 52/7 is often a little more aggressive in its appearance as it has 12/3 in its outer diamond, which is used more outwardly. It is more "pompous" and competitive and is more concerned with being on top, winning arguments and appearing strong.

Whereas the 25/7 energy turns things inside out and can have a tendency to have a kind of inner chaos, a rush of thoughts and a sense of victimization, this tendency is turned outwards in 52/7, which can create a lot of chaos in the environment in the form of an argumentative manner where everything is up for debate. Many journalists typically have this number because journalists are supposed to be curious and make their living by creating chaos and thus debate. So where 25/7 makes himself a victim inside without necessarily being able to see it, 52/7 makes others "victims" for through its teasing. Verbal aggression The argumentative appearance can be perceived by others as being aggressive, competitive and transgressive. You may feel as if the person is trying to create drama for drama's sake.

We are talking about a very extroverted form of 7 energy. As mentioned earlier, Neptune has the fastest winds in the solar system (figuratively speaking, a kind of chaos) and also likes to get below the surface to understand the context of things - something that can be done through the form of debate. This side of Neptune is especially emphasized by the outer 5 that stands for communication and 12/3 in the outer aura (formed by 5 and 7). 57/3 is an expansive energy that can both have a tendency to ignorantly overstep other people's boundaries, be clumsy with others' feelings and turn things upside down.

When we look at the diamond for 52/7 we see that it not only has 12/3 in the left aura between 5 and 7, but also has 12/3 as the essence number. It is built into the number that things in the outer world

must first be over-interpreted and turned upside down (as 12/3 tends to do) before it is turned around and put back into place by the inner 12/3 in the essence. So while this whole process will be done inwardly in 25/7 where thoughts and feelings are turned upside down, this process is done outwardly in 52/7 and often involves everyone else around them in the process.

This can sometimes frustrate others who don't understand why everything has to be discussed and turned upside down, because even when a discussion is seemingly undebated 52/7 can continue to debate. It's important to understand that this is part of the 52/7 energy process. Restlessness and close relationships The outer restlessness can also have consequences in marriage. 52/7 has a built-in tendency to build up a lot of misunderstandings especially with its close relationships, such as the spouse/girlfriend. You get bored quickly on a sexual level, and if you don't prioritize varying your lovemaking, this restless, freedom-seeking energy can lead to divorce. 12/3 energy can have a hard time with all kinds of "maintenance". It wants to grow and expand, but taking care of what is and maintaining it can feel "boring".

Since 52/7 is curious, you might think that it would want to vary its love life or create variety in its relationships, and of course it can if it wants to, often using other number energies present in the charts. But the curiosity of the 52/7 is often a more mental and non-committal curiosity than a "physical" curiosity that requires an active effort where you activate your love and your physical body. Traditional sources therefore attribute a risk of infidelity or broken relationships to 52/7 unless you actively seek to balance it with other energies. Another aspect of 52/7 is that, like 25/7, it is very international and travels a lot. However, travel should preferably be a voluntary thing

and something that is controlled by your own will and desire. However, there is a tendency for this travel to be due to external factors, so you can feel like a victim of all that traveling. This involuntary travel is something the 52/7s often attract unconsciously because they are restless within themselves and thus seek "restless" jobs that require them to travel abroad. Traveling around the world and sleeping in hotels and attending conferences and meetings, you risk becoming rootless - and of course this affects family life.

Problems with finances Unfortunately, this restless energy can also affect your business as you lose interest. You develop other interests and put off important business tasks. This prevents continuous growth as the mind is always elsewhere. This restlessness also makes it difficult to enjoy things as a 52/7 - either you can't figure out how to enjoy yourself or you never have the time.

### 61/7 THE ETHICAL PURIST

This vibration is about consciously letting go of the material world to move onto a spiritual path where you are filled with the joy of pure being.

In imbalance, there are too many principles of correct living that are imposed on others against their wishes and a lack of joy in life. In balance, one understands the deepest spiritual laws and is supported to live in a free, simple and unattached way. The vibration is not normally used in the name chart for new names as it can give a slightly "dry" vibe and is difficult to master.

The number is also sometimes called ”The Gandhi Number”. Mahatma Gandhi was small in stature but inside he was a giant - "Mahatma" which is the first part of his name means "the great soul" and Mahatma Gandhi also had a great mission.

Few can boast of liberating an entire country through principles such as "Ahimsa" meaning non-violence and "Satyam" meaning truth in speech and action. But Gandhi did.

Through his shining example, he got Indians to carry out peaceful strikes that completely paralyzed the British from running the country. When violence was brewing, Gandhi would go on hunger strike - and people would be so afraid of losing him that they would immediately stop fighting and violence. Even at the height of hostilities between the Muslims and Hindus, Gandhi would go on hunger strike for months and eventually they would stop fighting each other - such was the awe on both sides. And this is of course due to Gandhi's own example, but most of all because he channeled some divine principles through himself. Divine and eternal principles that are so deep and universal that they touch something in every human heart.

Voluntarily giving up what no longer serves you Where 16/7 is an energy where you often lose something or someone you love - 61/7 is a very conscious energy where you are able to voluntarily give up things or people that ultimately do not serve you or do not contribute to your further development. It is not uncommon for the 61/7 to feel drawn to renounce their family and enter a new life where they are free from family influence. In the deepest consciousness of the 61/7, they know that the deepest relationship in the world is between themselves and the divine.

Self-sacrifice What characterizes the 61/7 is that it is a number for self-sacrifice. You like to live like Mahatma Gandhi or Mother Theresa. You don't "belong to your family" but live for society and community and its highest good. You believe in simple living and high inner values. You don't wear expensive clothes and jewelry and even in your taste in food you show simplicity. You speak the truth and show truth in all your deeds. 61/7 also doesn't let others get away with speaking untruths or being immoral.

A respected and altruistic leader 61/7 makes you a respected leader. People come to 61/7 for advice and guidance - and people will naturally love to serve the 61/7 in gratitude for their deeds.

You like to be there for others and don't expect reward for what you do. You do it from a good heart and because you understand that self-sacrifice is a way to get closer to the divine.

You have all the universal and spiritual aspects of the 7 awakened. You are a symbol of a person of character and determination and can easily sacrifice your own personal comfort for the common good.

Very honest 61/7 is very straightforward and honest. You speak your mind and you hate being dishonest and disloyal. You also expect others to be like you - which can be a weakness - because other people don't always have the same high ideals.

Spiritual principles As a 61/7, you are comfortable with spiritual principles. You understand and live by them and thus become an example to others. Here are some principles to understand 61/7's inner mindset, which stem from the Hatha yoga philosophy that Gandhi actively followed:

- AHIMSA One should not show violence, neither in speech nor action (Gandhi's basic principle)

- SATYAM One should always strive to speak the truth - and also understand the many layers that lie in what the truth is. In truth is also an assessment of how much of the truth different people can bear to hear.

- ASTEYA One should not steal, and of course this applies to other people's possessions or intellectual property, but in the deepest sense one should not steal what belongs to God. We are born with nothing and go to the grave with nothing, so everything in this world is actually just on loan.

- BRAHMACARYA (sexual moderation): One should exercise moderation in one's sexual energy. One must learn not to desire what is not to be desired, but focus the energy of desire inward and upward.

- APARIGRAHA (minimalism) One should not overload oneself with physical things but make sure to weed out and have only what one needs around. This mindset is seen nowadays through the growing "minimalism" movement that comes as a backlash against the Western paradigm of always wanting more.

- SAUCHA (inner purity) You need to keep yourself clean inside and out. Outside in terms of physical purity, and inside in

terms of pure thoughts. Within the yogic tradition there are also a lot of physical saucha exercises such as tongue scraping, nasal irrigation, bowel irrigation etc. - exercises that cleanse the physical body and thus subtly also the emotional and mental body.

- SANTOSHA (contentment) You have a duty to be content with what you have and what is. You are where you are for a reason. Of course, you should not be passive if you are in a bad situation, but you should not focus your life energy on the bad and on what is missing. This teaching is very closely related to gratitude.

- TAPAS (refine yourself) The yoga practitioner performs a daily set of yoga exercises and meditations that improve the body's flexibility, ability to focus, mental concentration etc. Tapas helps to awaken the inner fire. It is said in esoteric texts that even a small action performed with great rigor on a daily basis without skipping a single day will ultimately lead to ultimate success.

- SVADHYAYA (studying spiritual texts) It is a virtue to read and study spiritual and sacred texts.

- ISHVARAPRANADANA (to be one with God) The ultimate goal of life is to be close to the divine source and strive to become one with the divine.
