### Finding the Right Registration Date

The first—and most important—step in creating a business numeroscope is selecting the super date on which the company will be registered. Typically, there are only 1–2 strong dates per month, and sometimes even fewer. Every year contains both good business dates and super dates. Good dates work well for projects, launches, and new initiatives. But when creating a new company from scratch, you always choose a super date.

Each year we calculate the super dates and plan all business activities—company registrations, major releases, offers, and strategic initiatives—around them.

#### What Defines a Super Date?

A super date is characterized by the following number combinations:

- Always aim for one of the numbers to be 23/5. This is the strongest business vibration.

- These numbers are excellent as DV (Day Vibration) or Life Path numbers: 19/1, 37/1, 23/5, 15/6, 27/9, 36/9

- These are good as Life Path numbers (but not as good as DV numbers): 17/8, 15/6, 24/6 Some example combinations:

- Essence number 23/5 with date 19/1 or 27/9

- Or the reverse: date 23/5 with essence 19/1, 27/9, etc.

- Life Path 24/6 with date 23/5

- Date 23/5 with essence 17/8

These are only a few examples of strong combinations between these key numbers.

## STEP 3: Personal Adjustments

It’s important to consider how the client’s personal numbers align with the company’s numbers.

This process is highly intuitive, and it’s not possible to define strict rules—but we can outline several guiding principles.

### RULE 1: Repetition Is Beneficial

If a client has certain key vibrations in their personal essence or numeroscope, these will be supported when the same numbers appear in the company’s numeroscope—for example 13/4, 17/8, 21/3, and others.

### RULE 2: What Is the Purpose of the Business?

If the client works with healing or with people, a 24/6 essence might work even better than a 23/5.

This was the case in several sessions where the choice stood between 23 August and 23 September. Clients working with healing and loving energies were a better match for 23 September, where the Life Path number was 24/6.

### RULE 3: Do They Have a Strong or Gentle Essence?

If the client already has a naturally fast-moving, strong, or dynamic essence, they can also choose a more powerful business date. But if their personal essence consists of softer energies, they may be better supported by a gentler date— for example a DV5 day with a 2 Life Path, instead of a DV1 day with a 9 Life Path. A strong essence typically includes:

- The World Conqueror numbers in the Higher Self

- Masculine numbers 1, 3, 9

- And possibly 8, which brings inner strength Use your intuition in the same way you do when creating a personal numeroscope.

- Masculine numbers: 1, 3, 9

- Neutral/Mental numbers: 4, 5, 8

- Feminine numbers: 2, 6, 7 Example: Choosing a Date A client had DV2 and 20/2 as her Life Path.

### Balancing Business Numeroscopes

In the standard settings of Numerologist PRO, certain numbers appear in red in the Higher Self and the Psyche, which we abbreviate here as HS and PS.

The numbers shown in red within a company’s essence should not be understood as negative.

They are reminders for the numerologist that these energies can be advantageously balanced by adding specific numbers to the numeroscope. These balancing numbers help the company express its most positive potential. Below are the most important balancing techniques used in business numerology: 11/2 in HS/PS Add 33/6 or 27/9 on the name line if possible.

If this is not feasible, place these numbers as Personality/Hearts Desire numbers or in the under-vibrations—or both.

12/3 in HS/PS Ideally, choose a numeroscope with 37/1 on the name line or as the Pillar.

If that isn’t possible, include 37/1 as a Personality/Hearts Desire number.

The number 21/3 also helps balance 12/3. It can often be placed as an under-vibration in individual names. It rarely appears as a Personality/Hearts Desire number—but if it does, it works well. 16/7 in HS/PS Make sure to include 27/9 on the name line.

If that doesn’t succeed, place it in the Personality/Hearts Desire positions or as under-vibrations—or both.

#### 4–8 Conflict in HS/PS

If a person or business has both 4 and 8 energies in HS/PS, it is important to include 37/1 in the numeroscope.

Normally this is easy to achieve, but if for some reason it isn’t, include 37/1 as a Personality/Hearts Desire number. You can also use 73/1, the inversion of 37/1, as an alternative. 18/9 in HS/PS In business, 18/9 can work well as one of the date numbers in HS/ PS if the owner already has strong 9-energy in their birthdate. ### Summary

This has been a short guide to selecting superdates and creating a personally adjusted numeroscope for the client.

The reason we place so much emphasis on superdates is because we have analyzed more than 1,500 companies—both successful and unsuccessful.

Our findings show that the registration date has a far greater influence on business success than numerologists have traditionally assumed.

Even a mediocre numeroscope can work exceptionally well if the business is founded on a superdate with strong supporting numbers. Of course, the ideal scenario is when both the date and the numeroscope are strong. Because numerologists historically have not given the full date-energy the attention it deserves, many people are walking around with an excellent business numeroscope— but founded on a suboptimal date.

You will therefore often meet clients who already have a good business numeroscope, but whose business was registered on a less optimal date.

By changing this date to a new and stronger one, they can receive far greater support on their path to business success. In business numerology, it is also essential to have optimal subnumeroscopes that support the company’s purpose—this will be covered in a later module.
