The 5 Bodies

”Pancha Kosha” or ”The Five Bodies” represents one of the

oldest conceptualizations of human consciousness experience

and offers us a profound perspective.

As human beings, our existence encompasses five interconnected

layers: the spiritual, mental, emotional, etheric (energetic), and the

physical. These layers are not only present throughout the universe

but are also mirrored within us, signifying a holographic reflection of

the cosmos.

The Spiritual Body/Bliss Body (Anandamaya Kosha):

This represents our ”consciousness body” and is the most subtle

layer of our being. We touch upon it in fleeting moments of pure

bliss, where we experience a profound sense of oneness with universal

consciousness.

19

The Mental Body/Wisdom Body (Vijnanamaya Kosha):

Composed of wisdom, this body encapsulates our knowledge, values,

beliefs, and inner sense of discernment. Awakening this body

enables us to perceive beyond the surface of reality, enhancing our

intuition and deepening our understanding. This body contributes to

creating our sense of identity or ”ego.”

The Emotional Body (Manomaya Kosha):

This body is shaped by our mind and heart and houses our perceptions

of the world, ourselves, and others. Within this layer reside our

emotions and behavioral patterns, influencing our interactions and

responses.

The Etheric Body (Pranamaya Kosha):

This body is made of energy and represents our life force or prana,

which flows through the circulatory, lymphatic, and nervous systems.

It is through this body, in conjunction with the emotional body, that

our aura is primarily formed – emitting energies that others unconsciously

perceive in our presence.

The Physical Body (Annamaya Kosha):

Formed of our flesh and bones, this body is literally made from the

nourishment we derive from the food we eat. It represents our most

vulnerable layer, as imbalances from the other layers can manifest

in physical ailments and disharmony.

Together, these five bodies provide a comprehensive framework for

understanding the different dimensions of our human experience,

shedding light on the intricate interplay between our physicality, energy,

emotions, thoughts, and consciousness.

The concept of the "psyche" originates from ancient Greece and

is deeply connected to the understanding of the soul. The word

"psyche" is actually the Greek word for "soul", reflecting the close

connection between the psyche and the soul in Greek philosophy

and mythology.

In Greek mythology, Psyche is personified as a mortal woman whose

love story with Eros (Cupid) unfolds into a journey of transformation

and self-realization, symbolizing the evolution of the soul and its union

with love. This story illustrates how the psyche (soul) undergoes

trials to achieve immortality and a deeper understanding of itself and

the universe.

In philosophical terms, Greek thinkers such as Plato and Aristotle

further developed the concept of the psyche. For Plato, the psyche

was immortal and constituted the true essence of a person, which

differed from the physical body, which was perishable. The psyche

was the seat of reason, courage and desire, which together formed

the basis of morality and ethics. Aristotle also saw the psyche as the

INTERPRETATION OF AURA NUMBERS

An aura number will always affect the number or numbers it stands

with, adding its own energy to the main number. The main number

will always be either a name, LT or GV. These will always be stronger

than the aura number, so it is the aura number that is the subordinate

- the one that adds an "extra color" or "timbre" to the main number.

An unbalanced aura number

If you have a balanced name value, but it is next to an unbalanced

aura number, the aura number will draw out some of the negative

aspects of the name.

For example, if your name is 24/6, which in itself is balanced, and it

is next to 12/3, then 12/3 will draw out the drama in 24/6. It will typically

be about failed love relationships and drama in relationships/

family life - which is already an aspect of 24/6 unbalanced. It will also

make you feel like a victim (12/3) of these relationships. It can also

mean that you feel that you need help from others (an unbalanced

aspect of 24/6) and feel victimized (12/3) on that account.

The aura number adds its own tone to what the main number is

about, drawing out either positive or negative aspects of it.

A balanced aura number

If a balanced aura number, such as 14/5, is next to the main number

24/6, which is itself balanced - then 14/5 will boost good communication

(14/5) in relationships and family life (24/6), and provide even

more networking and help from others, which 24/6 is about in balanced

form.
