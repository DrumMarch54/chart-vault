## **Number 4 as the love life aura**

The number 4 has two aspects - it's solid and creates a framework, but every now and then it

can suddenly find a way to break the framework.

The number 4 spreads a practicality and stability that helps to take care of the partner and

little respect for the decisions of a group of people. Sometimes the 4 has better ideas -

sometimes it is out of a need to be non-conformist - and sometimes there is no reason other

than an inner satisfaction in always being at odds with others. This gives an energy of

anarchy - of going against the established order. This contradictory tendency will be felt

especially by family and loved ones. If the energy of 4 is out of balance, you may also

become the black sheep of the family, where you may find that you are excluded - or

sometimes not liked - the family simply does not understand what you are trying to say or

what you stand for.

4 in this position can also give a tendency to infidelity, although not as much as if it were 13/4

or if the number 4 was in the solar plexus. This comes from the 4's tendency to feel that the

grass is greener elsewhere than where you are.

Sometimes you may also feel that you are way ahead of your family or close relationships -

you feel that you have grasped concepts and seen or understood things that they could

never understand.

As the number 4 represents transformation and renewal - or always starting over - you may

have had the experience in your life of building a relationship only to have it fall apart and

you have to start over with a new person.
