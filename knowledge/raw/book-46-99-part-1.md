#### Apollo

Apollo is the god of the sun, light, poetry and song in Greek mythology. He is therefore often depicted with a lyre in his hand. Ancient Greek poems are known for the many rules that dictate rhythm and meter - how syllables are arranged by weight, number and duration. This connection to poetry also makes Apollo associated with moderation and "luminous wit".

When mentioning Apollo, thoughts often turn to Dionysus, who is

seen as a kind of opposite to Apollo. Dionysus was the god of wildness and passion. Apollo is therefore considered to be "nice" compared to Dionysus. Apollo is the gilded son. The shining "splendid specimen" who conquers the powers of the subconscious through consciousness, art, morality and oratory.

These qualities are also seen in people who carry this energy. Speaking prowess 46/1 gives great powers of speech and a deep understanding of rhetoric. People with 46/1 energy speak in a way where their intonation and rhythm support their speech to the maximum.

Barack Obama is a prime example of this. His speeches are rhetorical textbook examples, his voice is pleasant and full of authority. He weighs his words in a way that communicates wisdom and thoughtfulness. At the same time, there is "meter" in his voice - there is pressure and pauses in the right places. Several rhetoric experts believe that Obama's success is due to his voice and rhetorical skills. Luminous aura There is also something luminous about 46/1, like an almost "golden glow" in the aura that gives a great charisma and a kind of "shine" to the person.

#### Cleopatra

The number 37/1 is sometimes called the "Cleopatra" number, but in reality Cleopatra had the 46/1 energy as her surname "Philopator" gives 46/1.

Cleopatra is one of the most well-known women from history and famous for her beauty, intellect and love affairs with Julius Caesar and Mark Antony. She became Queen of Egypt after the death of her father Ptolemy XII and is often portrayed by Hollywood as a glamorous femme fatale.

According to many sources, Cleopatra wasn't actually that beautiful. It was more her intelligence and conversational skills that gave her her immense charisma.

We think of Cleopatra as Egyptian, but she was actually Greek and a descendant of one of Alexander the Great's generals "Ptolemy". The Ptolemaic empire she ruled was actually a Hellenistic empire that had taken over Egypt and continued the pharaonic tradition. Cleopatra spoke several languages - Latin, Greek and even Egyptian. None of the Ptolemy Pharaohs had bothered to learn Egyptian. Through her great intellect, strategic acumen and charisma, she remained Queen of Egypt for over 20 years - quite an achievement considering the great upheavals of the time.

Cleopatra was a shining example of the 46/1 energy. Political, talented, great charisma and an ability to use her skills to the maximum to achieve political success. 46/1 The glamour image 46/1 wants to radiate a "glamour image".

You have the perfect life, the perfect home, the perfect family. In its more unscrupulous form, this number sometimes also called ”The Apollon Politician” uses his/her family as a kind of parade to show that he/she is the "perfect family person". The family is used for a political purpose, or some other kind of agenda where it is important to radiate the perfect moral and political values.

A good example can be found in the political series "House of Cards" with the character "Will Conway", who is a Republican politician running against the main character Frank Underwood in the US presidential election. Will Conway exudes great strength and shine, but also has some of the more discordant aspects of 46/1. Conway and his wife are young and good-looking and are not shy about using these charac-

teristics to their advantage. The Conways make a big deal about showing their "family values" as seen in his frequent use of social media, where he appears with his wife and children.

Conway is also the "perfect son" - right after he graduates from high school, he starts law school and then joins the US Air Force - with the intention of giving vent to his political ambitions. His grandfather was also a decorated World War II war hero.
