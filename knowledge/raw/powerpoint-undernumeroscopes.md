#### SUBVIBRATIONS

#### • Subvibrations and Core Numbers in Pythagorean Numerology

- Subvibrations are the consonant and vowel numbers in each individual name.

- They amplify specific aspects of the name’s main number:

- Example: A 32/5 name with consonant:16 and vowel:16 will enhance the deeper, more introspective and thorough aspects of 16/7, making the usually outgoing 32/5 energy more inward and profound.

- Example: A 32/5 name with consonant:17 and vowel:15 will boost charisma, visibility, and the "celebrity" energy of 32/5 even more.

- Names with the same total value can feel very different because of their unique subvibrations.

- Therefore, it is important to understand these subtle differences when reviewing name lists.

- Personality Number and Heart's Desire Number (sum of all consonants and all vowels across all names):

- These are key numbers in Pythagorean numerology.

- They are stronger and more influential than individual subvibrations.

- Their impact can be felt very quickly, especially after changing names. SUBVIBRATIONS

#### • Personality Number → How others perceive us and our outer personality.

- Heart's Desire Number → Our private, inner space and how we experience life on the inside.

- Both are important:

- Personality Number → Outer personality, somewhat adaptable.

- Heart's Desire Number → Inner experience, more sensitive and deeply felt.

- Calculating Big Personality Number:

- Add all consonants in the full name.

- Example: ALBERT EINSTEIN → Total consonants = 28/1

- Calculating Small Personality Number:

- Calculate consonant sum for each name separately → reduce to single/double digits → add these sums.

- Example: ALBERT (11/2) + EINSTEIN (17/8) → 10/1 (sum of reduced totals).

- Calculating Big Heart's Desire Number:

- Add all vowels in the full name.

- Example: ALBERT EINSTEIN → Total vowels = 18/9

- Calculating Small Heart's Desire Number:

- Calculate vowel sum for each name separately → reduce → add these sums.

- Example: ALBERT (6/6) + EINSTEIN (12/3) → 9/9 (sum of reduced totals). HEARTS DESIRE AND PERSONALITY

#### • Personality Numbers

- Reflect how you act in the world and how others perceive you.

- If this number does not align with you, you may feel empty or disconnected.

- There are no strict rules for the "right" Heart's Desire Number — it is about what supports you emotionally and inwardly.

- Big Heart's Desire Number → Shows long-term direction of your inner wishes.

- Small Heart's Desire Number → Shows your everyday inner feelings and desires. HEARTS DESIRE AND PERSONALITY
