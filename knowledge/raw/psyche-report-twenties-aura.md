## THE NUMBER 5 IN THE TWENTIES

A 5 in the twenties aura indicates that you were lively and that you enjoyed life and being with your peers. There was probably more contact with thoughts than with feelings. The defence mechanism if you felt threatened may have been to speak quickly or to deceive your opponents. Communication skills meant you were probably good at bringing different friends together and helping them connect with each other.

## THE NUMBER 6 IN THE TWENTIES

A 6 in the twenties aura indicates that you had a very charismatic aura, and you probably turned a few heads here and there. The number 6 is good in your twenties because it helps you to capture your future spouse! It's not that you have to settle down right away, but it's likely that you met your current spouse during this period, as it marks the peak of sexual charisma.

## THE NUMBER 18 IN THE TWENTIES

The twenties could have felt like a struggle, constantly having to fight and prove oneself. With an 18/9 aura, there is a strong theme of masculinity in the twenties. There may have been very strong authority figures that you had to live up to or prove yourself to. You may have been in situations where you had to fight with your peers to prove yourself. You might have grown up in a very competitive environment -or there might have been others who were very hard on you, or you had to be very hard on others.
