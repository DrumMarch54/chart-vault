## STEP 1: PURE MASTER NUMEROSCOPES – THE FOUNDATION FOR THE NAME CHANGE

Your New Name Numeroscope – The Heart of the Name Change
The numeroscope is the foundation of a successful name change. It must be strong,
supportive, and balanced—while also resonating deeply with the individual’s essence and
life path.

### Main Numeroscope Checklist

- Choose the most relevant templates and add them to the client’s profile. The strongest and most commonly used templates are already included, labeled “Standard” or “Special.”
- Once you’ve selected a template, assess whether any numerical values need adjustment or if swapping middle names is necessary.
- Consider if reversing or reordering the names would enhance the overall harmony.
- Check for energies from the Higher Self (e.g., 11, 12, 4-8, 16, 18) that might need balancing.
- Are there specific name wishes from the client? If so, do they create too much of a compromise?
Year Rows – Tracking Energy Over Time
The year rows reveal how numerological energies unfold across the years. We use them
to ensure a harmonious flow and to avoid clusters of karmic or challenging years.

### Year Rows Checklist

- How many minus points does each template receive due to karmic periods?
- Are there several tough karmic years in a row? (If so, consider choosing another template.)
- Is the person a 4 or an 8? Be aware that combinations like 4-8-4 or 8-16/7-8 naturally appear more often due to their base vibration.
- Have you intentionally selected a numeroscope with a Life Expression (LE) 4 or 8, or a Past Life Resonance (PLR) 9? If so, these karmic periods can be excluded from the minus point calculation—just remember to untick 44/8, 88/7, and 99/9 in the assessment.

## STEP 2: PURE SUBNUMEROSCOPES – CREATE GREATER HARMONY AND BOOST THE EFFECT OF

THE NAME CHANGE
Subnumeroscopes – Supporting the Core Transformation
The subnumeroscopes complement the main numeroscope and should be kept as strong,
clean, and aligned as possible. They refine the energy of the full name and can make a
crucial difference in overall harmony.

### Subnumeroscope Checklist

- What are the highest-scoring setups available?
- How does the score shift when the client’s name wishes are included?
- Would a different surname (e.g., a newly created one) improve the results?
- Can the desired first name be optimized—either by tweaking the spelling or turning it into a double name?
### Weighting Principles for Subnumeroscopes

- Top Priority: Personality Numbers and Heart’s Desire Numbers carry the most weight and should always be strong and supportive.
- Next in Line: Aim for balanced center main vibrations—these stabilize the name’s core energy.
- Acceptable as Secondary Vibrations: Numbers like 4, 8, 9, 11, 13, 15, and 17 are semi-balanced and may appear in secondary center positions.
- Conditional Acceptance: The number 12/3 can sometimes be accepted in auras or as a center main vibration, especially in charts with Day Vibration 4.
- Avoid if Possible: Numbers such as 16/7 and 18/9 should be excluded whenever possible.
### Pro Tip

Aim for high scores and green indicators—but never at the cost of straying from the
person’s true essence or heartfelt wishes. The best results come when numerological
strength aligns with soul resonance.

### Evaluate Personality and Heart’s Desire Numbers

- Do you feel good about these vibrations?
- Do they reflect who you want to become or how you want to be seen?
- Take a moment to tune into the energetic feel of the different options. What resonates most deeply?

## STEP 3: SINGLE NAME CHARTS & NUMBER PYRAMIDS – FINE-TUNING EACH PART

Fine-Tuning the Full Name – Strength in Every Part and the Whole
Each name is adjusted to ensure that both the individual components and the full name
vibrate with clarity, strength, and energetic coherence.

### Checklist for Full Name Optimization

- Check the Number Pyramid for each individual name—aim for green indicators.
- Review the Single Name Chart for insights, but let the pyramid weigh more heavily in your assessment.
- Consider the total number of letters, which influences the “extra” Life
Expression.

- Remove all spaces in the full name and assess the combined Number Pyramid.
Pay special attention to the last three digits—they form a kind of “diamond” that
reflects the full name's concluding energy.

### Intuitive Exercise

Tune into the name—first each part, then the full name as a whole.
Try swapping middle names and observe how it affects the overall number pyramid.
You’ll often feel the shift immediately—trust that intuitive response.

## STEP 4: NAME DESIGN – CREATE YOUR OWN NAME

Designing the Final Name – Where Feeling Meets Precision
This is the creative and intuitive phase where you craft a name that feels right—and is numerologically
clean and aligned. It’s where soul meets structure.
🇩🇰 Practical Notes for Denmark
(Refer to the Name Law Manual for country-specific details)

- Newly created names (both first and last) typically take around 9 months for approval, as they must pass through the Family Law Centre.
- Newborns are exempt from this delay when naming for the first time.
- During the waiting period, we recommend choosing a temporary name with similar energetic values. These can be selected from pre-approved lists and are usually approved by the local parish office within 1–3 business days.
### Design Principles

- The full name must harmonize with the main and subnumeroscopes.
- The name can be unique and deeply personal, but must also be energetically balanced and clean.
- If you have specific name wishes, experiment with spelling variations and creative combinations.
### Tips for Creating Surnames

- Use your intuition and creativity. Draw inspiration from meaningful themes:
Nature, forests, stars, light, joy, fairy tales, Nordic traditions, vintage elegance, nobility, etc.

- Choose a language style (e.g., Danish, French, English).
- Play with suffixes:
- English: -ton, -field, -ford, -more
- Danish: -berg, -høj, -lys, -rup, -strup, -gård
### Tips for Surname Approval

- Avoid names that resemble protected names—in pronunciation or spelling.
- The more original and distinctive, the higher the chance of approval.

### Tips for Compound First Names

- Small spelling adjustments can significantly shift the vibration.
- When combining names, consider raising the target number (e.g., from 23 to 32 or 41) if you're struggling to find viable combinations. A higher target gives you more flexibility with consonants and vowels.
- Compound first names can go up to a numerological value of 55.

## STEP 5: NAME CHANGE TIMING – CHANGE PERIODS AND KARMIC RELEASE

Timing the Name Change – When the Energies Align
The moment you officially change your name is more than just a date—it's a powerful energetic shift.
Choosing the right timing can make the transition smoother and more supportive.

### Checklist: Transition Periods

To ensure a supportive shift, review the energies during and around the name change period:

- Avoid karmic vertical periods across the transition (e.g., 44/8, 48/3, 84/3, 79/7, 97/7, 88/7, 99/9).
- Avoid karmic horizontal periods, if possible, around the transition time.
- Avoid unbalanced numbers (12/3, 16/7, 18/9) in the bottom-left corner of the chart during the switch.
- Avoid vertical stress lines (9–11/2) especially around the new year or transition month.
- Don't wait too long—ideally, the change should happen within 4–5 months. It’s better to act than wait endlessly for the “perfect” date.
### Understanding the Karmic Release

The karmic release marks the energetic culmination of your name change—it’s the point where the old
patterns are fully released and the new name takes hold.

- Never let the release fall in the same year as the name change.
- The karmic release always occurs on December 31st—the energetic closure of the year.
- Identify the first karmic release number after the name change date—unless a mild release is immediately followed by a strong release. In that case, the stronger release is considered the true karmic release.
### Types of Karmic Release

### Mild Release

- 4, 8, 11/2, 15/6
### Heavy Release

- 12/3, 13/4, 16/7, 17/8, 18/9
### Alternative Drop

If no clear karmic release appears within 4 years, look for:

- A 7 or 9 in the left column near the transition period, or
- A horizontal karmic period in the final year line.
### Reading the Theme of the Release

The nature of your karmic release can be interpreted through the numbers at the end of the year (final
vertical diamond).

- The vertical number is the core release theme,
- The horizontal numbers act as supporting energies.
