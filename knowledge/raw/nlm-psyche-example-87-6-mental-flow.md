**87/6 Mental Flow created from the outer Psyche mental strategies of 71/8 and the inner Psyche mental strategies of 61/7:**

**This is an advanced interpretation focusing on the cognitive architecture of the psyche. The "Mental Flow" diamond is a crucial composite number, created here by fusing the conscious (Outer) and subconscious (Inner) mental strategies, resulting in 87/6, The Sorcerer.**

The calculation for the mental flow number of **87/6** is derived from concatenating the digit sum of the Outer Mental Strategies Diamond (**71/8** → 8) with the digit sum of the Inner Mental Strategies Diamond (**61/7** → 7).-----I. Inner Mental Strategies: 61/7 (The Ethical Purist)

The Inner Psyche Mental Strategies Diamond (61/7) reflects the deepest, often subconscious, cognitive framework and principles guiding your thought process.Context of 61/7 (Inner Mind)

The archetype associated with 61/7 is "The Ethical Purist" or sometimes referred to as the "Gandhi Number".

**In Balance:**

* **Spiritual Principles:** The mind is focused on consciously letting go of the material world to move toward a spiritual path, striving to be filled with the joy of pure being.
* **Conscious Detachment:** This is a conscious energy that voluntarily gives up things or people that no longer serve one's growth or further development.
* **Integrity:** The inner self is characterized by being straightforward, honest, and holding high spiritual principles (simplicity, philanthropy, spiritual laws).
* **Wisdom and Introspection:** The inner mind seeks to understand the deepest spiritual laws and resides in a place of spiritual integrity.

**In Imbalance:**

* The mind becomes prone to **anxiety and fear of loss**.
* The cognitive output is **judgmental and moralizing**, imposing too many rigid principles on others against their will.
* The psyche can appear **dry, unworldly, and detached**, often wanting to escape the world which is perceived as a hostile place.

-----II. Outer Mental Strategies: 71/8 (The Radiant Magician)

The Outer Psyche Mental Strategies Diamond (71/8) represents the conscious ego strategy developed in adult life, showing how the mind engages and acts upon the external world.Context of 71/8 (Outer Mind)

The archetype associated with 71/8 is "The Radiant Magician" or "The Illusionist". This is an inversion of the 17/8 "Star" energy, focusing on control rather than simply channeling divine energy.

**In Balance:**

* **Control and Charisma:** The conscious strategy is to have **total control** over charismatic and magical aspects, creating mesmerizing products or creations with deep finesse and fine-tuning in preparation.
* **Perfectionism:** The external mind has an inner diamond of 81/9, driving it to work **extremely hard** and seek a high degree of perfectionism, ensuring all details work together to create the *perfect illusion*.
* **Subtle Influence:** The individual operates powerfully from **behind the scenes**, influencing surroundings through charisma and charm.
* **Intellectual Inquiry:** The mind is deeply occupied with the question: **What is illusion and what is reality?**.

**In Imbalance:**

* The strategy can lead to **negative spotlight, selfishness, and belligerence**.
* There is a danger of **falling for one's own illusions**, mixing reality and fantasy in an unhealthy way.
* The individual may be **egotistical, individualistic, and lonely** and may develop **narcissistic or psychopathic tendencies** when extremely unbalanced.

-----III. Mental Flow: 87/6 (The Sorcerer/Wizard)

The Mental Flow, **87/6 (The Sorcerer)**, represents the synthesis of the inner moral filter (61/7) and the outer performance/control strategy (71/8), defining how the total conscious-subconscious cognitive process works.Interpretation of 87/6 Dynamic

The number 87/6 is an occult vibration, known as "The Wizard," who masters both the outer and inner worlds, but keeps its abilities more hidden and attuned to societal norms.Balanced Flow: Ethical Mastery and Intelligent Influence

The 87/6 flow channels the ethical depth of the inner 7 into the practical power of the outer 8, creating an influential, grounded consciousness:

1. **Principled Ambition:** The inner commitment to spiritual laws and truth (61/7) provides a profound ethical compass for the external Magician (71/8). The result is a Sorcerer (87/6) who operates with **high personal integrity, ethics, and morals**, using their mastery of universal laws wisely.
2. **Strategic & Eloquent Creation:** The Wizard's core ability is to get things done in a **relaxed, elegant, and super-intelligent way**, leveraging strategic sense and eloquence. The deep inquiry into illusion vs. reality (71/8) combined with the moral purity (61/7) enables **amazing storytelling skills**, allowing the individual to communicate complex, valuable ideas in an enchanting way.
3. **Controlled Charisma:** The natural magnetic presence of the Magician (71/8) is reinforced by the underlying spiritual depth, creating a strong and magical radiance. This radiance gives personal power and influence, making the individual effective in politics, law, or creating societal change.

Imbalanced Flow: Selfish Manipulation and Dark Occultism

If the inner ethical foundation (61/7) collapses into fear and judgment, it corrupts the Magician's control (71/8), leading to the severe shadow of 87/6:

1. **Psychopathic Control:** The rigid principles of the Purist (61/7 imbalance) combine with the controlling illusionist (71/8 imbalance) to create a terrifying mental strategy: **manipulative, dark, and dangerous**. 3. **Spiritual Reversal:** Instead of the 61/7 fulfilling its spiritual calling, the power of 87/6 turns inward and uses its strength selfishly, resulting in **"reverse kundalini,"** where energy focuses downward on the lower centers of physical pleasure, money, food, and power.
4. **Emotional Chaos:** The inner conflict can manifest externally as **chaotic energy, strange moods, and bizarre outbursts of anger**, blaming others for mistakes and creating drama in the environment. This inability to reconcile internal principles with external demands may also result in profound **depression and mysterious illnesses**.
