### Number 1

The number 1 gives you an innate drive and enthusiasm.

Celestial Body

1 is the number of the Sun. The Sun shines its light on all the planets giving life to the entire solar system. The Sun provides power, optimism, drive to be the best, enthusiasm, leadership skills, and a huge inner desire to be self-reliant and free.

Characteristics

- When the number 1 energy flows freely, it gives birth to creativity – like a river that surges forward, and that needs freedom, creativity, movement, and expression.

- Number 1’s are born with an inner “gold medal” around their necks because they are the “best” – and as a 1, you most probably want others to tell you that you are good and the best at what you do.

- Discussing anything with you might be a bit difficult because you have an inner stubbornness not allowing you to veer away from something you already have said. Admitting mistakes is not easy and feels like conceding defeat. This stubbornness often leads to other people seeing you as aloof or arrogant.

- As a 1, you most probably love luxury and might have a tendency to love beautiful and expensive clothing, fine dining, fancy cars, and jewelry. Many number 1 men, for example, like to wear gold watches, rings, gold chains around their necks, etc. and will invest a lot of money in buying expensive accessories for their loved ones.

- As a 1, you are good at formulating your ideas and especially if you have other people who can do the “dirty work” or heavy lifting.

- Luck naturally comes to the number 1 as they symbolize the energy of the beginning. Precisely for this reason, it becomes so difficult for a number 1 if he/she experiences no longer having luck or flow in his/her life.

Career

The number 1 is the born entrepreneur, willing to put all in to become the best at whatever he/she does. Number 1 wants to rise to the top of society and typically occupies the positions of the top leaders, politicians, entrepreneurs, lawyers, etc.

Love Life

As a number 1, you will be naturally attracted to people who can feed your ego and fetter you. For this reason, you also easily experience jealousy as you desire to be the center of attention.

The Most Significant Emotional Issue to Greatest Liberation

- The biggest emotional issue of the number 1 is vanity, arrogance, narcissism, and an inability to take in advice or feedback.

- Imbalanced number 1 is dictatorial, stubborn, arrogant, angry, power-hungry, and overly perfectionistic, setting too high demands on others.

- Balanced number 1 is connected to source energy and full of infinite power and enthusiasm.

- Aspire like the Sun to be unaffected by the clouds in the sky. Despite shifting moods and emotions, you will carry on shining with a brilliant inner power.

USE YOUR POTENTIALS

Find your passion

You're naturally hard-working and determined - but when you also find your passions and focus on them in your work, you also get into a strong flow that gives you an unstoppable power that can take you really far. Feel what's really exciting and irresistibly interesting to you and focus on that.

Be radiant like the sun

It is the deepest nature of a 1 to shine and radiate like the sun. Be a role model for others in the things you are really good at and never hide your light under a bushel. Shine your light on others, whether in the workplace or in your personal life. It's not your job to run around doing what others say, catering to their needs, and hiding your own light. Your greatest task as a 1 is to constantly follow and amplify your own inner light, and you do this by working purposefully with your passions, following your inner spark, and being a pioneer full of courage and leadership.

Tap into your creativity and pioneering spirit

You are a visionary who has come to contribute something unique and luminous. Learn to listen to your vision and follow your inner voice. You can think big and out of the box. When you awaken the high, balanced side of the 1 energy, you access unique innovation, creativity and originality. There is an almost cosmic creative spirit flowing from the center of your being. This can be channeled into creative work, as well as ideas for making the most of your personal life or your career. You can easily think innovatively, originally and practically at the same time.

Your Spiritual Power

The number 1 resonates with the wisdom that all is one. As a 1, you deeply understand wholeness and can feel at one with the entire universe and all living beings. This is your greatest spiritual power. In this wisdom you awaken your heart chakra and become a force of light and love. At the same time, it gives you enormous popularity, where other people are naturally drawn to your personal energy. The positive feelings, thoughts and moods you create in others will rub off on you many times over.

TURN YOUR WEAKNESSES INTO STRENGTHS

Keep the Peace

You have a tendency to get upset and stressed, to get too busy, and to take on too many tasks. You can prevent this by relaxing your body and emotions every day. Incorporate some yoga, walking, or meditation into your daily routine. Plan your activities realistically, and say no to things that aren't important, that you don't like, or that aren't your core competency. Instead, outsource them to others who love them and are good at them.

Watch out for angry emotions

When you feel pressured, you may tend to project your anger, judgment and negativity onto others. Beware of being controlled by "angry" emotions such as aggression and rage, which are the energy of the Sun in imbalance. Giving free rein to these emotions can damage both your health and your reputation. As a 1, you need to be loved and liked. Focus on communicating in a way that is respectful to others, even when you feel angry and impatient. Instead, use your "fire," your drive to achieve your goals.

Don't be too self-centered

As a 1, you can become self-centered, so always remember to connect with the whole - this is also one of your spiritual strengths, as the number 1 symbolizes unity and all being one. For example, remember that your innate talents are a gift from the higher powers. Don't take credit for everything in life. Cultivate gratitude and humility - and you'll receive even more from the cosmos that already is bestowing you with great blessings.

Do not play the victim

It doesn't suit anyone to be a victim, but as a 1 you are least made for it. If you find yourself using a victim role to get something, such as a discount on a service, extra good service, help, or attention, then you know you're barking up the wrong tree. Of course, you can ask others for help when you need it. But be careful when you use victimhood to "bargain" for some kind of extra service.

Tendency to dominate

Beware of being too dominant. Others are not always as strong as you, but still have something to offer. Remember to give others space and ask for their opinions, views, ideas, etc. A good leader considers the input of others. Work on your contempt for others if you find yourself looking down on people.

Mute your criticism

You can be critical of others as well as yourself. Learn to forgive yourself and others, and accept that it is human to fail. Let go of the negative feelings that can easily arise when you think something is not quite good enough. Other people rarely love criticism, and they may come to hate you a little - whether you are right or not. Formulate your feedback to others in terms that are very constructive, and let people feel that you are coming from the heart and that you understand their perspective. For every point you want to criticize, find at least two things you think are great. Also, use your self-criticism positively to make things as good as they can be, and accept that you are where you are.

Equal partners

It can be tempting for 1s to choose partners who are inferior to them in some way in order to feel superior and in control. But that doesn't always lead to the best results. When choosing partners in business (as well as in love), it's important to find some who are as skilled - or even more skilled - than you are, so that you have some competent people with you. When you are able to get feedback and advice from others, you have overcome something in your 1 ego, and you will be rewarded many times over in the form of even more success, happiness, flow and balance.

The path to good self-confidence

You have a side that is full of confidence and a side that is low in confidence, and you may well swing a lot between the two. Remember, you don't have to prove yourself all the time to be loved and appreciated. You can just be who you are. Sometimes others like you better when you're not the master performer all the time. As a 1, you may have a hard time learning something new and want to skip the step of being a beginner and go straight to the step of being a master. But that's not the way reality works. Be patient and only compare yourself to yourself. You'll get good at things, be patient with yourself. In balance, your number 1 drive is big and your ambitions are high. If you're not doing successful and exciting things and initiatives, it's pretty much because your confidence is low. If you are even noticing conditions like laziness and idleness, and are tuning out of your own life with time wasters like too much TV, excessive gaming or social media, etc., then you are off track. Work consciously on your thoughts and learn to use positive affirmations to build your confidence. If you are out of balance, this is the best way to get back into balance. When you are in balance, you are focused, confident and empathetic. When you're out of balance, you lack all three.

Watch out for addictions

In imbalance, the 1 can tend to become addicted to various things that are all about energy levels. Whether it is gambling, too many city trips and parties, or acting out going crazy, possibly combined with alcohol, drugs, etc. Remember, it's about your need to feel the rush of being in contact with large amounts of energy. Instead, you need some exciting projects where you can use your large amounts of energy so that it doesn't accumulate and create frustration and addiction. Instead, use it to create personal success. Focus on your ambitions, recognize your need for success, and use your energy to create something positive so that you can get your "kick" out of what you create.

Asking for something

In your relationships, you may tend to be passive, for example, waiting for your friend or partner to guess your needs. If you don't like the direct approach, which many 2s do not, find a more elegant and gentle way to express your needs.

Share your feelings without going into them

Some 2s find that others get tired of their need to talk about their feelings. One key to making it easier for others to listen to you is to practice being honest about your needs and feelings without expressing them in the moment. For example, you can tell your partner that you are upset about something he or she is doing, but without expressing it through tears, crying, or blaming. Practice expressing and sharing your feelings without going into them. It works best if you wait to share your feelings until you have processed them yourself. That way, other, less emotionally oriented people will understand you more easily, and you'll get more of what you really want: to be heard, to be given space, and to be understood. Remember that many others are not so well versed in the realm of emotions, and will often react in the opposite way to what you want when you express yourself with great emotional intensity.

The tendency to be submissive

This tendency can manifest itself in several ways. It may be part of your personal charm at first, but over time it becomes a habit that causes you to always underestimate yourself, your seriousness, and your abilities, thus giving away your power. Examine whether you are underestimating yourself or making yourself submissive in some areas of your life. Focus on the fact that you can be gentle, soft, accommodating, flexible, and kind, and still have a sense of boundaries, a good sense of self, and be strong in yourself.

Secretly, they want to be discovered for the amazing person they are and receive all the love they send out in return. But again, this is a dream. When you are in balance, you are inspired, confident and empathetic. When you are out of balance, you lack all these qualities.
