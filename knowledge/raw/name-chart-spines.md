## Key: 3-5-6-7-8-10-11-12-15-18

## 3/3 AS SPINE NUMBER

The number 3 is a good number to have as a spine number and means that potential

problems and difficulties in life will be solved with a mindset of success, humor, strategy and

strength.

The number 3 also gives you a good "poker face" - that is, you don't show what you're really

thinking and feeling. This is a good skill to have when, for example, you have to negotiate

and you don't want the other person to know how you feel. It gives a good ability to bluff your

way to the desired goal.

However, this strength can become a weakness when there are problems in your personal

life, because there is a tendency with the number 3 that instead of opening up and telling

how you feel, you deny that there are problems and shut other people out.

## 5/5 AS SPINE NUMBER

With 5 as your spine number, you try to solve the problems that arise in your life through your

network. You contact your friends and acquaintances to see if they know someone who

knows someone. And if you can't find anyone who can help you, you tend to get quite mental

and start thinking about things very in a chaotic and frantic way. And if thinking about the

problem doesn't help either, then the 5 can end up becoming manic, ungrounded and run

around in circles.

The 5 energy can feel very helpless in trouble because it relies heavily on persuading others

to help and often lacks the ability to get things done on its own. Fortunately, the number 5

gives a good ability to network, so you will probably eventually find someone who can help

you.

## 6/6 AS SPINE NUMBER

With the number 6 as your spine number, you try to solve problems through charm and by

creating harmony in your surroundings.

With the number 6 it's very much about people, so you like to solve problems through

diplomacy and by talking directly to people and then using flirtatious charm to convince the

person who might be causing problems to change their behaviour. Or create alliances with

others who can help go against the person or entity causing the problems.

In imbalance, there can be a tendency towards passivity, powerlessness, laziness and

stubbornness, putting off and not addressing problems.

## 7/7 AS SPINE NUMBER

With a 7 spine, one will have a natural tendency to deal with the problems in life by trying to

see them from all angles. One will seek to understand the deeper underlying nature of the

problem before solving it, rather than recklessly throwing yourself at it.

7 has a natural tendency to express universal love, so you would not act aggressively

towards a problem, you would first try to solve it in a way that was loving and fair to all people

who might be involved in the problem.

There can also be a tendency to solve problems by analyzing and going in and getting a

mental overview. If you are spiritually inclined, you might try to meditate on the energy of love

or visualize your way to a solution. If you are professionally inclined, you might study the

literature to find out how other people have solved the problem.

7 is a very transcendent and gentle energy and can easily be unbalanced by discordant

numbers in the chart. In this case, the unbalanced 7 energy in the spine can cause a

tendency to ignore life's problems (like the ostrich burying its head in the ground), to

procrastinate, to accept or to be overly concerned.

## 8/8 AS SPINE NUMBER

With an 8 spine, problems are solved through patience and persistence. The 8 energy knows

how things work on the physical plane and has an arsenal of methods to use. When faced

with problems in life, there is a tendency to turn up the volume on the methods used so far,

but the problems may continue when the old solutions no longer work.

In imbalance, the number 8 can be very inflexible, intolerant and traditional, so that one can

stare blindly and only use old methods and systems that have worked in the past, but may

not work in a new situation or a new time where newer and more innovative solutions are

needed. In many cases it would be better to implement something completely new or turn the

whole situation upside down to see new angles and approaches to solving the problem.

The 8 tends to hold a grudge when someone does something they don't like. Instead of

solving the problem with the person, they don't say anything but punish the person by

excluding them. The next time the person approaches, they are met with a wall of silence

and hostility.There is a tendency for the 8 to not ask for help when they have problems, but

to put on a stoic face and pretend that everything is fine.

## 10/1 AS SPINE NUMBER

With a 10/1 spine, you solve problems by showing leadership and optimism. However, you

must be careful not to become too extreme in the methods you find to solve problems, or be

so manic or fanatical that other people fall away and begin to hate you.

There can also be a tendency to be perfectionistic and over-critical with the 10/1 energy.

When problems arise in life, there can be a tendency to criticize others for their mistakes, but

forget to see the mistakes you made that caused the problems to arise.

## 11/2 AS SPINE NUMBER

Unfortunately, 11/2 is not such a good spine number. There is a tendency to be indulgent

and exude an energy of weakness and indecision.

The spine is about how you deal with life's problems and challenges, and complacency,

indecision and weakness do not make for a healthy and strong spine. Instead of tackling life's

problems head on, there will be a tendency to retreat, hide or procrastinate.

On the positive side, 11/2 in a well-balanced name chart will act as two pillars in a temple,

both helping to keep the roof up - a number that allows you to see both sides of any problem

and find a win-win solution for all parties involved. People will work on problems as a team

and solve them together.

## 12/3 AS SPINE NUMBER

With 12/3 as the spine number, there will unfortunately be a tendency to feel like a victim of

life's problems. One thinks too much and acts too little. Or you think too little and act too

much.

There can also be a tendency to endlessly twist and turn the options and end up doing

nothing about the problems that arise.

You can also have an incredibly bad sense of the situation and come up with solutions that

don't fit the scale of the problem at all. For example, if it's a small problem, you make the

problem big in your head. If it's a big problem, you might not see it at all and start talking in a

way that either offends people, makes people think you're a bit of a clown, or makes the

the problem worse.

With spine number 12/3, it's important to be aware of your timing. You shouldn't be too trigger happy, but you shouldn't wait forever either. The art is to forge while the iron is hot.

## 15/6 AS SPINE NUMBER

With 15/6 as the spine number, the natural tendency is to meet life's challenges by focusing

on beauty and sensuality.

If 15/6 is unbalanced by disharmonious numbers in the chart (such as 11/2, 12/3, or 18/9), it

can mean that you react to life's challenges with drama or fits of rage, and that the smallest

problems are enough to make you flee completely and be overdramatic.

If there are a lot of 8s or 9s in the chart, the 15/6 energy becomes cooler and evaluates

challenges from a more astute and intelligent place, looking at the pros and cons of a given

situation and also assessing the strengths and weaknesses of the opponent. This is where

15/6 can really show its strength through strategy, tactics, cunning, and guile.

## 18/9 AS SPINE NUMBER

Spine number 18/9 gives you a tremendous drive and power to carry out any action you

start. Problems and trials in life are also solved with an extreme masculine and martial power

that goes straight to the heart of the problem.

The 18/9 energy gives a strong will, determination and independence in the way one deals

with life's challenges.

You are likely to have a very straightforward style, easily telling people what you think and

how you think things should be done - and you can sometimes be so "overly honest" that you

potentially hurt others.

With a 18/9 spine, you sometimes have to be careful not to overdo it - you can potentially

become too fanatical or jump to conclusions. Sometimes it is better to WAIT and take things

slowly when faced with problems, allowing space for the best solutions to emerge.
