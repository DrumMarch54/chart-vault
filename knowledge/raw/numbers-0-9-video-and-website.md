0 AS SYMBOL

Zero in numerology is a symbol for Cosmos, the space or void that lies as a backdrop for everything else. It is the transcendent and timeless aspect of the divine, which lies beyond time and space, while at the same time in a mysterious way also being time and space itself. It is everything, and yet it is nothing. This view of the zero and cosmos is related to the view of the universe in modern physics. Since it takes positive energy to separate two pieces of matter, gravity must be using negative energy to pull them together. Thus, “the gravitational field has negative energy. In the case of a universe that is approximately uniform in space, one can show that this negative gravitational energy exactly cancels the positive energy represented by the matter. So the total energy of the universe is zero.

The shape of the 0 also implies wholeness. It contains everything within it, and yet it is empty – a void. It is infinite because you can carry on going around it for ever, and yet it is contained and in “one place”.

ETHER

The circle shape in symbology represents the awakened heart center, which sends its energies equally to all sides. The elongated circle shape that we see in zero is also a symbol for ETHER.

Ether is the substance of which space-time is made off. Like waves in water propagate through the water, light waves in space propagate through the ether.

Space is not “empty” but a void that is full of creative potential.

Today, physics tells us that there is hardly any difference between “mass” – what we perceive as objects in space and space itself. Everything in the universe is actually “space.”

FEEDBACK LOOP

Specific directions in physics claim that both gravity and electromagnetic energy are aspects of the void energy or space itself. Gravity is space itself, which spins and pulls everything in towards a singularity where information is processed – and electromagnetism pushes the energy out of the singularity but now in a new and higher form.

The process of life itself is a function of the feedback loop of the universe. The probability of life arising without a guiding “intelligence” from the universe is infinitely small. Here we have the famous airplane quote from the scientist Hoyle

A junkyard contains all the bits and pieces of a Boeing-747, dismembered and in disarray. A whirlwind happens to blow through the yard. What is the chance that after its passage a fully assembled 747, ready to fly, will be found standing there?

“The chances that life just occurred are about as unlikely as a typhoon blowing through a junkyard and constructing a Boeing-747.”

Hoyle calculated that the odds of 2000 proteins and 200 amino acids spontaneously coming together to create life would only happen in 10 ^ -40000 of times.

Life can only arise because the universe is helping it to arise through a self reflective intelligence where all experiences are drawn back into the energy of the void, processed and emitted again in a new and higher order.

The energy of space is continuously learning from itself through an endless feedback loop. The whole universe is a constant fluctuation between singularity and becoming. Black holes are singularities where space is “spinning,” creating what we see on the surface as gravity, pulling in matter. The black hole then hurls the matter out again as electromagnetism. Black holes – or singularities – lie at the heart of everything in the universe. The proton is a singularity. The human being is a singularity that resides as a center point between an outer world and a hidden inner world. Our sun is a singularity that lies at the heart of our solar system. Every system has a center or a singularity. In the zero symbol, we see both the singularity – the space in the middle of the zero where experiences are synthesized, and we see time and space, which is the periphery of the zero where the experiences themselves take place.

The Zero energy is the energy of the fluctuating void, always creating and always reabsorbing, learning, and processing. The zero is the point of assimilation that takes in everything that has transpired in 4-dimensional space-time, processes it, learns from it, and restarts the whole process again. The universe is a conscious, intelligent, and living organism, always learning and always evolving through experience through endless feedback loops. The middle of the zero is the consciousness aspect. The periphery of the zero is the material aspect, a screen upon which the consciousness projects all its learning processes.

THE TAROT CARD FOR 2

The High Priestess sits with a thin veil decorated with pomegranates. The veil represents the distinction between the conscious and the subconscious realms, the seen and the invisible - and aims to cover the more profound mysteries from the eyes of the uninitiated. Only the initiated person can enter into this sacred space.

The pomegranates on the veil symbolize abundance, fertility, and the divine feminine. They refer to the story of Persephone, who ate pomegranate seeds in the underworld and was forced to return there yearly.

On each side of the priestess we see two pillars that mark the entrance to this sacred mystical temple (also connected to the Solomon Temple). The pillar to the left is black with the letter B (Boaz meaning "in his strength"), and the one to the right is white with the letter J (Jachin, meaning "he creates"). The colors of the pillars symbolize duality - masculine and feminine, dark and light - which symbolizes that knowledge and acceptance of duality are necessary to enter the space of the hight priestess.

The high priestess wears a blue robe with a cross on her chest and a diadem (or crown). These are symbols of her divine knowledge and her status as a divine ruler. In her lap, she holds a scroll upon which is written the word "TORA," referring to the underlying and most profound law of the universe. The scroll is partially covered, symbolizing that this sacred knowledge is both explicit and implicit, it will only reveal itself when one is ready to look beyond the material realm. The crescent moon at her feet symbolizes her connection with the divine feminine, her intuition and subconscious, and the moon's natural cycles.

THE TAROT CARD FOR 3

The tarot card is called the Emperor and is a symbol of the Father or the paternal principle. The Emperor sits on a large throne made of stone, which is surrounded by four ram heads - a symbol of his connection with Aries and the planet Mars (the number 3 which symbolizes Jupiter has the 9 number which symbolizes Mars as its essence number).

NUMBER 4: "The builder"

The number 4 gives you the ability to build new things from the ground up and to think inside AND outside the box.

Celestial body

4 is the number of Uranus. Uranus is a unique planet in the solar system. It has a horizontal axis of rotation, so it practically "rolls" along with its path around the sun like a wheel – as opposed to all the other planets that have a vertical axis of rotation.

Characteristics

Like Uranus, a four is entirely it's own. You might, therefore, have a unique music style, style of clothing, and view of life.

In Uranus, winter is long and lasts 21 years, which symbolizes that the 4 goes in-depth with things and is thorough and thoughtful. This ability to go deep and to think profoundly gives Uranus an amazing talent for innovation, and some of the most significant scientists and geniuses have many 4's in their charts.

The shift from winter to spring on Uranus creates great upheavals and devastating storms until the new season establishes itself. The same thing applies to the life of the number 4, where you might experience significant changes that mean losing everything and starting again from the ground up. For this very reason, number 4 is called a number of fate or karma.

The number 4 can also experience significant contrasts within themselves. They are at the same time, gentle and sensitive, but have a strong survival capacity, which can give an almost opportunistic nature.

The four is the number of the square – a reliable and robust shape – but with the same desire to build something "square" and stable also comes a deep and sometimes hidden willingness to tear it down again and to start again from scratch. This tendency is not so evident in the pure number 4 energy but is more apparent in the 13/4 and 31/4 variants of the four.

This deep and for many 4's hidden tendencies to go against well-established structures once they are built can give the 4 a rebellious and anti-authoritarian streak, continually saying and doing the opposite of others and going against the main consensus stream – this may lead to the 4 quickly feeling lonely and overlooked – like the black sheep in the family.

Uranus is the quietest of all the planets. Similarly, a 4 will not reveal so much about itself. They can be very talented and successful, but do not show it - contrary to Jupiter or the sun that are eager to show their success.

Career

The 4 energy gives a deep and sincere desire to build new structures and frameworks, a real pioneering spirit - and you most probably have a practical sense that allows you to get things done. You also have a great perseverence that allows you to take on jobs that others would never be able to complete. While other number 4’s (such as 13 or 31) are more mental and contrarian in their nature the pure number is the most stalwarth of the 4’s, being at the same time steady and firm.

Love life

4's are amazing lovers full of creativity and passion. If somebody transgresses their boundaries, they can shut off and become ice cold. So beware of this tendency! Also, beware of being overly critical of your love partner.

Something that can drive partners crazy is your contrarian nature. Often when somebody says something to the number 4, the 4 responds, "No – I think it should be like this "– whereafter, you say the exact same thing as was suggested. Sometimes the number 4 feels the need to say "no" to create a reframing, a shock effect, or a way to create a pause in the conversation that allows new energies and angles to come into play. But for others having to deal with this contrarianism, it can be not very easy.

Biggest emotional issue to greatest liberation

Your most important lesson in life is to open your heart to humanity and feel that you belong. When the 4 energy is imbalanced, it can make you feel wrong and different, dissatisfied, bitter, misunderstood, and that life is meaningless like a cog that doesn't fit in anywhere.
