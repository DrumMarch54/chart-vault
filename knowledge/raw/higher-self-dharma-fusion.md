The Dharma fusion numbers, also known as the Dharma number and Dharma diamond in Chaldean numerology, represent the crucial communication pathway between the conscious and subconscious minds.

This document explains how the fusion of conscious and subconscious minds occurs, followed by a compilation of specific Dharma fusion numbers and their corresponding details.Fusion of Conscious and Subconscious Minds

The Dharma diamond describes the communication between the month vibration (conscious mind) and the year vibration (unconscious mind). The ultimate goal of this communication is the fusion of these two minds.

- The Merging Process: When there is good and pure communication between the conscious and unconscious, they merge and form the "supramental mind".

- The Result (Supramental Mind): This state of the supramental mind allows the deepest secrets of the universe to be revealed.

- Spiritual Context: Many spiritual traditions suggest that merging the conscious and subconscious minds awakens the 'supraconscious' mind, a level of consciousness in touch with divinity.

The Dharma diamond provides numerologists with insight into how to communicate with God/the universe and how to live one's inner spiritual life. There are 81 different Dharma diamonds, each representing a unique path to success.Specific Dharma Fusion Numbers

The fusion of the conscious and subconscious minds occurs through a specific Dharma Number, calculated as the sum of the digit sums of the month vibration and the year vibration.Dharma Number 4/4
