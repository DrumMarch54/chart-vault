## CASES

### Why Are Cases Important?

#### Practice Through Real-Life Examples

Cases are essential for honing your skills in interpretation. While there are 99 core numbers, the psyches of individuals are infinitely nuanced. People express their numbers in unique ways, and the charts reveal not absolute paths but potentials—highlighting tendencies, probabilities, and the likelihood of certain behaviors or thoughts. The more cases you analyze, the deeper your understanding will grow of how to interpret the various charts and unlock their potential. For instance, interpreting an Essence Chart differs significantly from interpreting a Psyche Chart, as each chart provides insights into different aspects of a person’s life and personality.

### CASE: Donald Trump

#### Donald Trump, born on June

14, 1946, in Queens, New York, is an American politician, businessman, and media personality.

He gained fame as a real estate mogul, building the Trump brand through luxury properties and hosting the reality TV show The Apprentice (2004–2015).

Trump entered politics in 2015, winning the 2016 U.S. presidential election as a Republican. His presidency (2017–2021) was marked by tax reforms, immigration restrictions, and controversial policies like withdrawing from the Paris Climate Accord.

He was impeached twice but acquitted both times. After losing the 2020 election to Joe Biden, Trump remained influential within the Republican Party and won the 2024 election, becoming the 47th U.S. president in January 2025.

Trump’s legacy is polarizing— praised for economic policies by supporters but criticized for divisive rhetoric and controversies. He remains a significant figure in American politics and culture.

### CASE: Taylor Swift

Taylor Swift is a globally renowned American singersongwriter who has revolutionized pop music since her debut in 2006.

Born on December 13, 1989, she began her career in country music before transitioning to pop, becoming one of the most successful artists of her generation. Swift is famous for her exceptional songwriting, which often draws from personal experiences, and her ability to reinvent her musical style. She has won numerous awards, including multiple Grammy Awards, and holds several world records.

Her albums like ”1989”, ”Folklore”, and ”Midnights” have topped charts worldwide, and her Eras Tour in 2023-2024 became the highest-grossing tour in history.

Beyond her music, Swift is known for her cultural impact, business acumen, and advocacy for artists’ rights. She has successfully re-recorded her earlier albums to regain control of her music, becoming a billionaire and a powerful figure in the music industry. Her storytelling, musical versatility, and connection with fans have made her a defining artist of the 21st century.
