## Recap: What karmic connections can you have?

Karmic connections are dictated by whether you share Destiny Rhythms. But beyond that, there are a few different types of connection depending on the nature of the relationship.

## Long-term karmic connections - family, close friends, love partner etc.

When you have the same Destiny Rhythm as living relationships in your family, love partner or close circle of friends, there will always be a karmic connection.

The karma may well be dissolved, so it becomes a supportive relationship. Or the karma can be very unprocessed, making the relationship difficult and energy consuming - until it is resolved and the relationship is healed. This can happen in this life or in a future life.

## Particularly strong karmic connections

When the same numbers are shared in The Higher Self in addition to Destiny Rhythms, this is a particularly strong karmic connection. In some cases, twin souls can also be involved.

## Short-term karmic connections - romances, colleagues etc.

You may also experience short-term karmic connections with colleagues, romances etc. that are intense in nature, be it positive or negative.

A short-term relationship will typically die out as soon as the karma is dissolved, as the relationship will no longer be energized.

## Deceased and future family members

When you share the same Destiny Rhythm as deceased members of your family tree, as well as future members of your family tree far into the future, there is also a kind of karmic DNA where you are connected across time.
