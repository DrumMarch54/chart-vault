**Cosmic Laws of the Numeroscope (Chart)**

The numeroscope (the name chart) is the most sacred of diagrams from ancient Chaldean numerology. This chart has been kept secret for thousands of years and passed on between teacher and student through a deep and mysterious oral tradition. The chart resurfaced 30 years ago in Scandinavia, where it now is widespread to get numerological consultations and change one's names to create more flow and harmony in one's life.

The deep mysterious power of the numeroscope is due to some of the following factors:

**1) MICROCOSMOS MACROCOSMOS**

The chart resembles the figure of a human being. There is a power in graphical representations that correspond to the human body as it awakens the laws of microcosmos macrocosmos and the laws of correspondence. The law of microcosmos macrocosmos is essentially the law of the holographic universe. All information in the universe is stored in the parts. The whole body energy system's imprint is holographically mirrored in the hand, the foot, the iris, the ear, etc. In the same way, the numeroscope chart reflects the human being on a smaller scale.

**2) THE LAW OF UP AND DOWN**

In the universe, there is "up" and "down." The "up" in the heavens, the universal laws, the higher realms. The "down" is the physical world filled with physical objects. This "up" and "down" is mirrored in the human being through the laws of microcosmos macrocosmos.

The higher self chart (the triangle on the top) is based solely on the numbers in our birth date. These are the energies, karma, and potentials that our soul brings with it, as it is the soul that chooses our date of birth through complex processes of resonance. The higher self tells a story about our spirituality, soul aspects, and deepest yearnings. Here the memory of past lives is stored. The higher self is most active in our childhood before the energies of our names start vibrating with a "louder" frequency. As we pass our middle ages and enter old age, the higher self reawakens as we again become close to the spirit.

The human self chart is created by how our names vibrate with our birth vibration and represent our current incarnation. Our names vibrate with our higher self in a specific way that creates all the emotional and physical experiences that we encounter in our life. The human self chart tells about our love life, desires, career, and fundament in life. The human self chart can, in turn, be separated into up and down. The upper part of the human self chart tells about the health in our upper body, our values, our mental processes, outer brand, and cashflow. The lower part of the human self chart tells about our lower body's health, our self-esteem, our emotions, love life, and sexuality.

**3) THE VISIBLE AND THE HIDDEN**

Everything in the universe has something outer and visible, which is revealed to those who gaze upon the thing or object. But everything also has a hidden side, a side that resonates with mystery and the unknown. The left part of the chart represents that which is visible, the front side of our body. The right part of the chart represents that which is hidden, our "back," the hidden aspects of ourselves.

**4) THE LAW OF VIBRATION**

Everything in the universe is governed by vibration. Nothing describes vibration more accurately than the numbers. Due to our number system's cyclical aspect and the laws of microcosmos and macrocosmos, our number system can holographically "absorb" or "mirror" all the energies of manifestation.
