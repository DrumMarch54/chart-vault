#### INTERPRETATION OVERVIEW

This interpretation ”recipe” gives a quick overview of the most important numbers and combinations. You can use this recipe when interpreting your own or another person’s 5 Body Numeroscope.

#### The Higher Self

Here you go through all the numbers one by one. Think about how you think the person uses their innate numbers, and is influenced by them in different ways in their life. Feel free to highlight what you think is most important, but go through the whole thing.

- What is the Day Vibration (DV) – what is the personality like?

- What are Big and Little Life Path Numbers (LP) - what is the person’s deepest essence and life purpose?

- What is the Month Vibration (MV) – how is the person’s conscious self (personality and external agency)?

- Outer Diamond – how does the person go to the outer world? The Outer Diamond is formed by Day Vibration digit sum and Month Vibration digit sum.

- What is the Year Vibration (YV) – how is the subconscious?

- Inner Diamond – how is the inner world experienced? The Inner Diamond is formed by the digit sum of Day Vibration and the digit sum of the Year Vibration

- Dharma Line/Dharma Diamond – when is there most possible flow, what is the key to success? The Dharma Diamond is formed by the digit sum of the Month Vibration and the digit sum of the Year Vibration.

- DV-LP diamond – how does the person’s outer and inner personality interact? The DV-LP diamond is formed by the digit sum of the Day Vibration and the digit sum of the Life Path Numbers.

- Target number - what should the person aim for in life to achieve the fastest and most fundamental success? The Target number diamond is formed by the digit sum of the Day Vibration and the Upper Aura digit sum. What does this target number tell about the persons fastest way to achieve liberation or “quantum jumps” in his/her spiritual development?

#### The Psychic Self

1) Comparing Psyche Life Expression Diamonds A good starting point is to compare the Outer Psyche Chart Life Expression Diamond with the Inner Psyche Chart Life Expression Diamond. This involves examining how the psychologies of each diamond:

- a) Support each other, and

- b) Potentially create conflict.

#### 2) Comparing Mental Diamonds

Now, compare the Psyche Mental Diamonds, which reveal how thoughts are processed and what drives verbal communication patterns.

#### 3) Comparing the Challenge Numbers

Now compare the Outer Psyche Challenge Number with the Inner Psyche Challenge Number: Navigating the Transition to Adulthood 4) Comparing Pillars in the Psyche Charts These numbers reveal how the psyche ”finds rest”— what it needs to feel stable, grounded, and function at its best. Compare the two psyche pillars and create a synthesis of how you feel the two psyche charts are grounded.

#### 5) Look at the Composite Chart

If one overlays the Outer Psyche Chart on the Inner Psyche Chart one can see the exchange of energies between the brain hemispheres. Do this by for example concatenating the Outer Psyche Heart number to the Inner Psyche Heart number, and the same with the Solar Plexus numbers. What do you notice.

6) Zoom out and look at the over all energies Try zooming out and viewing both charts simultaneously. Observe how energy flows throughout the chart. Pay special attention to energies entering from the left side—how do they move inward? For example, if the psyche chart challenge number is 11/2 (positioned on the left of the central line), imagine how easily external energies penetrate this person’s psyche, influencing the surrounding numbers. How do these energies continue to propagate through the chart? Consider how an emotion generated in the Life Expression Diamond of the Inner Psyche chart would express outwardly through the filtering lens of the Outer Psyche Life Expression Diamond. These were just some examples – relax your gaze and allow the numbers to inspire you.

#### INTERPRETATION

The Name Chart Here you look at the following positions and read about the numbers there.

Name line:

- What do the names tell about the personality?

- What energies/keys does the person have available to play on in their life?

- Do you think that the person predominantly lives out the balanced or unbalanced of the names? Feel free to highlight what you find relevant and can relate to what you know about the person.

The names show something about the person’s ”energetic digestion”. Look at the first name, middle names and last name and think about how the person processes energy that comes from outside (the first name), how it affects them inside (the middle names) and how their family karma (the last name) dictates that they must handle the energy - what strategies are needed to deal with energies that come from outside.

Life Expression and pillar: The Life Expression (LE) – what is life heading towards? Which way is life moving? What are you interested in in your life? What experiences do you attract in life? How does it make sense for this person to have this Life Expression? For example, can you see the Life Expression reflected in his/her career? Etc.

The Pillar (PL) – how is the foundation in life? Does the Pillar provide a solid foundation in life and, for example, a good career, or the opposite?

The centers:

- Heart center and the Heart Diamond - how are communication, thoughts and how is the person perceived?

- Solar Plexus and the Solar Plexus Diamond - how is the emotional life, the inner drive and the relationship with oneself?

- MC Diamonds (Middle Name Center Diamonds) – There will be as many MC diamonds as there are middle names. How do these MC diamonds affect the Main Vibration in the Heart Diamond and the Solar Plexus Diamond?

The Auras:

- Close Relationships Diamond (Lower Left Aura Diamond) – how is the area of close relationships affected?

- Friends diamond (Upper Right Aura Diamond) – how is the relationship with friends affected?

- Environment diamond (Lower Left Diamond) – how is the relationship with the environment affected? (Eg colleagues, local environment)

- Public diamond (Upper Right Diamond) – how is the relationship with new people and the public affected? How do you act on the outside?

The Sub-numeroscopes Personality numbers and Hearts Desire

- Look at Small and Big Personality numbers and feel what these tell about the person’s outer personality - what the rest of us see and notice.

- Look at Small and Big Hearts’ desire and feel what the person’s innermost dreams, wishes, longings and deepest motivations are about. Look at the Vocation and Personality Charts Life Expressions and pillars Are they good pillars that support the Personality numbers and Hearts Desire numbers?

The main energies in the Vocation Chart

- What is the Vocation Chart Heart Diamond, Solar Plexus Diamond, Small and Big Hearts Desire number. Write them down.

- For exam­ple, for Katrine Ditte Pilmark, they look like this: 83/2 to 48/3 to 15/6 and 15/6. This gives a sense of the energy flow in the Vocation Chart.

The main energies in the Personality Chart

- What is the Personality Chart Heart Diamond, Solar Plexus Diamond, Little Personality number. Write them down.

- For example, for Katrine Ditte Pilmark, they look like this: 52/7 to 39/3 to 8 and 44/8 This gives a sense of the energy flow in the Personality Chart

Look at the Centers Main Vibrations:

- Are they clean and open (eg 2,3,5,6,7,10,14,15)?

- Or the slightly harder numbers in the centers (e.g. 4,8,9,12,16,17,18)? Or the slightly more dramatic ones (e.g. 11 and 15)?

- Also look at the centers’ extra Vibrations to rate the center as a whole:

- Are there the two dramatic numbers 11, 15 which can trigger the person to become more emotional?

- Are the extra vibrations pure? Or are there the half-balanced numbers (4,8,9,11,13,15,17)? Or are there unbalanced numbers as extra vibration (12,16,18)?

- Use your intuition to judge the center based on whether the main Vibration and extra Vibrations together create a balanced center.

- Also look at the auras and the Heart Diamond from which the center is created.

Zoom out and see the whole When you have gone through all the parts of the 5 Chart Numeroscope, it is time to zoom out. Are there any common numbers/patterns between the different parts?

Are there, for example, the same numbers in The Higher Self as in the Name Chart, or between the Sub-numeroscopes and Psychic Self, etc.?

Just take a look at what you think about it all, and extract the things that you think best describe the person as you know/ perceive him or her.
