## 10/1 AS PSYCHE CHALLENGE NUMBER

When 10/1 is placed here in this position, it means that there may be a tendency to be restless, impatient, manic-depressive and confused. Instead of being a number of divine order and success, 10/1 makes you disorganized and you go down with high blood pressure and stress. You become critical and over-perfectionist (like the number 1) and either on or off (1 or 0) -everything is full throttle or nothing. For example, you can be a bit extreme in your opinions or manner, making you loved or hated by others. There can also be a tendency to be a bit over-perfectionist with the things you care about, which can make others angry. There can also be a tendency to be a bit manic with one's appearance. If you manage to get through this mental challenge, you can fully tap into 10/1's great winning mentality and easily get the things you want in life.

You become selfless, loving, helpful and highly empathetic. You become honor bound and expect this from others as well. You have a lot of EMPATHY and a profound KNOWLEDGE OF HUMANITY.
