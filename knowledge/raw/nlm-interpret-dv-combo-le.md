# interpret DV combo LE and big LE

To create a highly objective, zoomed-out summary, you want to use a simple three-part formula that captures the person's journey from their internal mindset to their ultimate destiny.

Here is the exact "lingo" you should use for this framework:

*   **The Day Vibration (The Core):** Use phrases like *"At their core, they are..."* or *"Consciously, they are driven by..."* to describe their ego and mindset.
*   **The Little Life Expression (The Reality):** Use phrases like *"...but their daily life constantly attracts..."* or *"...navigating an immediate environment of..."* to describe the physical reality they are pulled into.
*   **The Big Life Expression (The Evolution):** Use phrases like *"...ultimately pushing them to evolve into..."* or *"...building a long-term legacy of..."* to describe the highest evolutionary step and long-term potential of their soul.

When you put this lingo together, you get a clean, single-sentence objective summary. Here is how those previous examples look using this zoomed-out format:

**Example 1 (Day 12/3, Little LE 17/8, Big LE 80/8)**
"At their core, they are an analytical overthinker (12/3), constantly attracting high-profile spotlight situations in their daily life (17/8), ultimately building a long-term legacy of massive executive power (80/8).".

**Example 2 (Day 19/1, Little LE 15/6, Big LE 69/6)**
"Consciously, they are a radiant, independent optimist (19/1) navigating a daily reality of intense emotional drama and popularity (15/6), ultimately evolving into a master of profound healing and balanced love (69/6).".

Using this simple, three-beat formula—**Core $\rightarrow$ Reality $\rightarrow$ Evolution**—allows you to objectively sum up a person's entire life dynamic in just one breath.
