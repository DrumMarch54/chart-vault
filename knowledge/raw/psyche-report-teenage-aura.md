## THE NUMBER 3 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

The most important lesson with a 3 teenage aura was to learn to master your emotions and also to assert your personality in a healthy way. This teenage aura provides a great sense of humor that could probably make the others laugh. Your defense mechanism was to be smart and think faster than the others. If you felt threatened, you were probably good at putting on a mask or poker face and not revealing how you really felt.

## THE NUMBER 4 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

With a 4 in this aura, you probably had a good practical sense in your teenage years, so you began to understand how things work, both on a practical level, but also mentally. The 4 has an ability to understand complex systems and how the different parts of a system interact with each other. Emotionally, however, the 4 can have a hard time with other people. The defence mechanism is either to withdraw completely and feel misunderstood as a black sheep who is not wanted by others, or to be rebellious and anti-authoritarian. The number 4 provides a rather unique and counterintuitive sense of humour -where you can make weird jokes or say strange things that make people stop and think. So you might have this effect on your classmates.

## THE NUMBER 5 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

A 5 aura means you were a lively teenager who enjoyed life and socializing with friends. You were probably more in touch with your thoughts than your emotions. The defence mechanism if you felt threatened may have been to speak quickly or to deceive your opponents. Communication skills made you good at bringing different friends together and helping them connect with each other.

## THE NUMBER 8 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

An 8 aura here indicates that as a teenager you were probably quite strong, independent and good at managing your time. You were probably also a bit cautious. People weren't allowed to get too close. The defense mechanism was to exert some sort of control over the environment and make sure everyone behaved as they should. You probably observed the world around you to balance your inner expectations with what was actually happening in the world. If you knew through observation how people behaved and drew the right inner conclusions, you would also know better how to protect yourself.

## THE NUMBER 9 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

The 9 aura indicates that as a teenager you probably had a strong sense of justice and were good at leading others. Things were said very honestly and directly, which could sometimes make others upset or angry. The number 9 here can provide tests of strength in the teenage years , when you may have had to fight and stand up for yourself. You probably stood up for your friends as well. You might have been quite cautious about yourself and how much emotion you showed others. For example, if the cameraman said “smile”, you probably hardly flinched your mouth. If others tried to hug you and you didn't like it, you probably pushed them away right away without thinking much about it. You were safe if you were respected and others didn't get too close.

## THE NUMBER 10 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

The 10 aura indicates that as a teenager you were quite strong mentally and physically. You may have had a certain polarizing effect on the environment, which caused you to divide public opinion. For example, you may have had some friends who really loved you, and perhaps there were others who did not share the enthusiasm. There may have been a tendency for you to either be super on -actively spending time with and engaging with others -or super off, completely in your own world without bothering to engage with others. The defense mechanism was to show that you were the best at something, whether it was something academic, or a particular sport that you were good at.

## THE NUMBER 11 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

The 11 aura indicates that as a teenager you were probably very sensitive and could feel all the energies around you. It may have been difficult to speak up and you may have found that you were easily hurt. There may also have been a feeling that you could not defend yourself against others and that others were always overstepping your boundaries. You might have escaped into a self-created inner world to avoid interacting with other people.

## THE NUMBER 12 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

The 12 aura indicates that as a teenager you were expansive and mentally awake. The teenage years could have been difficult, however. You may often have felt misunderstood, as if people didn't quite understand what you were saying. Alternatively, you didn't understand other people's intentions, which made it difficult to navigate the social scene.

## THE NUMBER 13 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

The 13 aura indicates that as a teenager you were probably very original and perhaps seen by others as being a bit odd. You might have been a little awkward and introverted. This may have led to feelings of being the black sheep, or at worst that you were some kind of outcast. The defence mechanism in difficult situations could be either to withdraw or to stand up to any tormentors by being anti-authoritarian and rebellious. You probably had a head full of original ideas and new ways of doing things. The humour could have been quite original and perhaps even a little dark and scary at times.

## THE NUMBER 14 IN THE TEENAGE YEARS

The 14 aura indicates that as a teenager you were good at making new friends and talking to others. You were probably likeable and full of life. You probably enjoyed speed and were attracted to sports where some agility is required. You probably also had a quick mind and sense of humour and were likely interested in many different things. You probably didn't go super deep into any one thing, preferring to generalise your attention. The defence mechanism in difficult situations was to get everyone talking to each other -and if that got too difficult, you probably just went somewhere else. You could have had a "media addiction" -that is, you spent too much time in front of a screen.
