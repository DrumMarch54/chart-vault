- Principled Leader: Stands as a pillar of integrity, holding themselves to standards that inspire others to rise above their petty concerns.

Imbalanced

- The "Terminator": Can become robotic, cold, and utterly ruthless in their pursuit of the "end goal," trampling over emotions and people in the process.

- Nihilism: If the spiritual connection is lost, the focus on endings can lead to deep depression, hopelessness, and a sense that "nothing matters."

- Rigid Judgment: Can become the ultimate judge, jury, and executioner, cutting people out of their lives permanently for the slightest infraction.

- Catastrophic Force: In negative polarity, this vibration can attract or cause "doomsday" scenarios, total collapse, or massive destruction in their personal or professional life.
