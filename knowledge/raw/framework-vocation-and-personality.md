### Definition and Purpose

The Vocation Chart, also known as the "Heart’s Desire Chart" or "Soul Urge Chart," is a fundamental component of the 5 Body Numeroscope. It is constructed from the vowel values in a person's full name and their day of birth vibration. Its primary purpose is to map the inner world of an individual, revealing their deepest desires, secret longings, hidden motives, and how they prefer to spend their time.

The sources describe the Vocation Chart as a map of the heart's energies and the architecture of desire. It functions as an energetic map of the soul's interaction with life, translating the soul's voice into intuition, ideas, plans, innermost desires, and willpower. It is considered a bridge between the physical incarnation (represented by the Name Chart) and the soul (represented by the Higher Self and Psychic Self charts).

Key Functions:

- Reveals Inner Motivation: It shows the deep and hidden motives behind a person's actions. These are often energies kept private, including hidden desires or aspects of one's sexuality that one may not wish to share with the world.

- Acts as a Resonator: The numbers in the Vocation Chart are described as "resonators" that activate the law of attraction. By awakening and aligning with these numbers, an individual can attract their true desires into their life, creating a sync between their soul and physical existence.

- Defines Likes/Dislikes: It details what a person is attracted to, including hobbies, interests, people, and even preferences in food, art, and music.

Comparison to Other Charts:

- vs. Higher Self Chart: The Higher Self chart reveals the soul's chosen lessons and highest potential, representing a longing to become a certain "archetype". The Vocation Chart is more specific, attaching desire to outcomes that can be seen, measured, and felt in the physical world.

- vs. Psychic Self Chart: The Psychic Self chart shows the unfolding of psychic energies over a lifetime based on birthdate numbers. The Vocation Chart shows how these soul-level energies are modulated and channeled into worldly desires through the vowels of the name.

- vs. Personality Chart: The Personality Chart is built from consonants and defines a person's outer charisma and how others perceive them. The Vocation Chart, built from vowels, reflects inner desires, intentions, and will. The consonants give words their shape, while vowels are more subtle and hidden. The Vocation Chart is you "when you sit still and tune into what you want in life," whereas the Personality Chart is you "when you are in full motion and engaging with life and other people".

- vs. Name Chart: The Name Chart is the "conclusion" of the Vocation and Personality charts. It is the most physical chart, showing the concrete unfolding of life, while the Vocation Chart maps the inner life of hidden desires and intentions that precede physical manifestation.

### Structure and Calculation

The Vocation Chart is a numeroscope created from the vowels of a person's full name in conjunction with their Day Vibration. A key rule is that the augmentation rule is not used on the central line's values in this chart.

- Middle Name(s) Vowels: Placed in the center of the name line.

- Big Heart’s Desire Number: Calculated by adding the values of all vowels in the full name together, without reducing the individual name sums first. It reveals long-term desires and how they will unfold over time.

- Bottom Right Aura: Last name vowel digit sum + Little Heart's Desire digit sum.

4. Centers (Hearts and Diamonds): The centers are syntheses of the auras and represent the core mental and emotional desires.

- Vocation Heart Center (Mind Diamond / Heart's Choice Diamond): Calculated by adding the Top Left Aura digit sum and the Top Right Aura digit sum. This composite number represents one's deepest intention and the "Choice" aspect of manifestation.

- Vocation Solar Plexus Center (Emotion Diamond / Heart's Emotion Diamond): Calculated by adding the Bottom Left Aura digit sum and the Bottom Right Aura digit sum. It represents the "emotionalization" of the heart's choice and the "Desire" aspect of manifestation.

5. Pillar: The pillar is the foundation of the Vocation Chart, ensuring that one's desires are well-grounded. It stabilizes inner emotional life and affects the Heart's Desire numbers.

- Calculation: The pillar is found by adding all the digit sums in the "central pillar" of the numeroscope: Day Vibration digit sum + Heart Center main vibration digit sum (and sub-vibrations, if any) + Middle name(s) vowel digit sum(s) + Solar Plexus Center main vibration digit sum (and sub-vibrations, if any) + Little Heart's Desire digit sum.

## A Definitive Guide to the Personality Chart Framework

### Definition & Purpose

The Personality Chart, also known as the charisma chart, is one of the five core charts in the Chaldean numerology system. It is constructed from the consonants in a person's full name in conjunction with their day of birth. This chart's primary purpose is to define and map an individual's outward personality, charisma, and the "final form" they take in the physical world. It reveals the characteristics, traits, and qualities that one transmits to the outer world and represents the first impression given to others.

The sources state that "the personality chart is the map of the first impression you give to others, and for the most part, this is how far you let others get to know you". It shows what kind of people you like, your tastes, which facts you accept, and what you allow into your being. Ultimately, it reveals the aspects of yourself you share with others and the people, situations, or things with which you do not resonate.

#### How the Personality Chart Differs from Other Charts

Each of the five charts in the full numeroscope describes a different aspect of an individual's soul structure, psyche, and life manifestation. The Personality Chart's unique role becomes clear when compared to the others:

- Vocation Chart: The Vocation Chart is built from the vowels in the name and maps out your inner desires, intentions, will, and secret longings. The Personality Chart, derived from consonants, represents the outer expression or "form" given to those inner desires.

- Name Chart: The Name Chart is the large, central chart created from the full names (both vowels and consonants) and the day of birth. It serves as a "conclusion" of both the Vocation and Personality charts, showing how life unfolds in concrete, physical manifestation. It is the most "physical" of the charts, detailing the actual outcomes of one's actions.

- Higher Self (Essence) Chart: This chart is created solely from the birth date and reveals the soul's chosen lessons, karma from previous lives, and highest potential. It represents the spiritual layer, while the Personality Chart shows how that essence is projected outward through the filter of the name's consonants.

- Psychic Self Chart: Also created solely from the birth date, this chart maps the unfolding of psychic energies and mental development over a lifetime. The Personality Chart shows the charismatic expression of that underlying psychological structure.

### Sacred & Historical Context

The conceptual foundation of the Personality Chart is deeply rooted in ancient esoteric and linguistic traditions that differentiate between the hidden, inner world (vowels) and the structured, outer world (consonants).

The source material draws a direct symbolic parallel to Semitic languages like Chaldean, Hebrew, and Arabic, where words are traditionally written using only consonants. Vowels are the "breath" added during speech to give context and meaning, while the consonants provide the core structure and "form" to the word. In this framework:

- Vowels (Vocation Chart) reflect your inner desires, intentions, and will.

- Consonants (Personality Chart) define your outer personality, charisma, and the form you take in the world.

In the context of the five subtle bodies from the yogic tradition of "Pancha Kosha," the Personality Chart corresponds to the bridge between the Pranamaya Kosha (the etheric or energetic body) and the Annamayakosha (the physical body). While not a direct overlay, Kabbalistic tradition provides a complementary perspective. In Kabbalah, Hebrew letters (which are almost all consonants) are considered forces of creation. Each letter possesses a number, form, and sound expressing spiritual qualities and connecting to divine archetypes. The consonants are seen as representing "personality impulses and karmic pathways," reinforcing the idea that the Personality Chart maps the energetic forces that shape one's outer self and destiny.

### Structure & Calculation of the Personality Chart

The Personality Chart is built using the numeroscope framework, but its values are derived exclusively from the consonants of the full name and the Day Vibration.

Key Rule: The augmentation rule is not used on the central line's values in the Personality Chart.

Here is a step-by-step guide to its construction:

#### 1. The Central Line (Name Line)

The numerical values of the consonants for each name (first, middle, surname) are calculated and placed on the central line.

- First Name (Left/Emanating Side): The consonant value of the first name is placed on the left side of the name line.

- Middle Name(s) (Center): The consonant value(s) of any middle name(s) are placed in the center.

- Surname (Right/Receptive Side): The consonant value of the surname is placed on the right side of the name line.

Example (Mary Louise Streep):

- Mary (M, R): M(4) + R(2) = 6

- Louise (L, S): L(3) + S(3) = 6

- Streep (S, T, R, P): S(3) + T(4) + R(2) + P(8) = 17/8 These values (6, 6, 17/8) are placed on the chart's name line.

#### 2. The Life Expression Equivalents (Personality Numbers)

At the bottom of the chart are the two most important numbers: the Little and Big Personality Numbers. These are considered some of the most precise indicators of one's vocation and calling.

- Big Personality Number: This is the sum of all consonant values in the full name, calculated without intermediate reduction. It shows the person you aspire to become in the long run and what you become in the eyes of the world. It is placed in the large circle at the bottom of the chart.

- Example (M,R + L,S + S,T,R,P): 6 + 6 + 17 = 29/2.

- Little Personality Number: This is the sum of the digit sums of each name's consonant total. It represents the personality you project in your daily life, here and now. It is placed at the very bottom, in the Life Expression position.

- Example (Mary 6, Louise 6, Streep 17/8): 6 + 6 + 8 = 20/2.

#### 3. The Auras

The four auras are calculated by adding the digit sums of the names on the central line with the Day Vibration (for the top auras) and the Little Personality Number (for the bottom auras).

- Top Left Aura (Outer Mental): First Name Consonant Digit Sum + Day Vibration Digit Sum.

- Top Right Aura (Inner Mental): Surname Consonant Digit Sum + Day Vibration Digit Sum.

- Bottom Left Aura (Outer Emotional): First Name Consonant Digit Sum + Little Personality Number Digit Sum.

- Bottom Right Aura (Inner Emotional): Surname Consonant Digit Sum + Little Personality Number Digit Sum.

#### 4. The Centers

The two centers synthesize the energies of the auras.

- Heart Center (Mental Charisma): Top Left Aura Digit Sum + Top Right Aura Digit Sum. This represents your mental charisma—how others perceive you on a cognitive level.

- Solar Plexus Center (Emotional Charisma): Bottom Left Aura Digit Sum + Bottom Right Aura Digit Sum. This represents your emotional charisma—the feelings people perceive when they are in your intimate aura sphere.

#### 5. The Pillar

The pillar is the foundation of the Personality Chart, grounding the entire personality structure. A stable pillar awakens confidence in others, while an unstable one can make others uncomfortable. It is calculated by summing the digit sums of all numbers in the "central pillar" of the chart.

- Calculation: Day Vibration Digit Sum + Heart Center Main Vibration (+ sub-vibrations) + Middle Name Consonant Digit Sums + Solar Plexus Main Vibration (+ sub-vibrations) + Little Personality Number Digit Sum.
