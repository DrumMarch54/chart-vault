### THE NUMBER 1 - INTRO

The number 1 is the number of the Sun. It represents energy, vitality, optimism, being the best, confidence, and a strong charisma. Facts about the Sun: It is 4.6 billion years old and classified as a yellow dwarf star. A yellow dwarf is a medium-sized star in the main sequence of its life cycle, characterized by its yellowish hue due to the specific range of surface temperatures it maintains—approximately 5,000 to 6,000 degrees Celsius. Yellow dwarfs like the Sun are stable stars that generate energy through nuclear fusion in their cores, primarily converting hydrogen into helium. The Sun’s size and luminosity place it in the middle range compared to other stars in the universe—it is neither a massive giant nor a small red dwarf. However, despite being called a “dwarf,” the Sun is incredibly large by our standards, with a diameter of 1,392,684 km, making it 101 times wider than Earth. Its mass is equally staggering, being 333,000 times that of Earth. This immense size and mass make the Sun the central anchor of our solar system, providing the gravitational pull that keeps all the planets in orbit. The Sun’s surface temperature is 5,500 degrees Celsius. All of its energy is generated internally. Its core temperature is 15 million degrees, created by a fusion process where hydrogen is converted into helium. The Sun is enormous—960,000 Earths could fit inside it, and it contains 99.86% of the entire mass of the solar system.

The Sun is intense. Scientists say that when its fusion process ends, the Sun will transform into a red giant, consuming Mercury, Venus, and Earth, before collapsing to the size of Earth and becoming a white dwarf.

As a numerologist, these facts about the Sun aren’t something you need to memorize, but they help us tune into the consciousness behind the Sun. When we reflect on the Sun’s physical characteristics, it also tells us about the Sun as a form of consciousness.
