### The Psychic Self

The Psychic Self is a fascinating part of the 5 Chart Numeroscope, which helps you to look very deeply into another person and understand their motivations and reasons. You examine The Psychic Self after analyzing The Higher Self, in order to go even deeper. The Psychic Self consists of the two blue diagrams you can see on either side of The Essence Chart in the image down below.

While The Higher Self tells about your karma and your highest potential in life, The Psychic Self deals more with the inner psychic energies and how your psyche is built up.

The Psyche consists of two numeroscope charts. The key difference between them lies in the values placed on the central horizontal line. In the inner Psyche chart (corresponding to the right emotional brain hemisphere), the values on the central line represent the month value and the year value. In the outer Psyche chart (corresponding to the left rational brain hemisphere), the left value on the central line is derived from the Essence Chart Outer Diamond digit sum. If this sum is less than 10, the number 9 is added to it. The value to the right is the year value. We will explore the outer Psyche chart in a later section of this book. To simplify things, we will begin by focusing on the inner Psyche chart.

## THE OUTER PSYCHE

### The Outer Psyche Chart

Now, let’s create the outer psyche chart.

The outer psyche chart is constructed by adding the digit sum of the Day Vibration to the month number. If the resulting number is still under 10, we further add 9 to ”octavize” it, elevating it above 10. This unique adjustment, known as the Day Enhancement Rule, also applies to the Name Chart. The rationale behind this rule is that the Day Vibration carries such a strong energy that it influences these numbers. Additionally, numbers under 10 are considered ”childish” or ”receptive,” so the Day Vibration amplifies and enhances them by its influence.

The outer psyche chart is deeply fascinating to study because it reveals the pronounced effects of your Day Vibration on your psyche. With the inner psyche chart, we observe how the psyche would naturally develop in a more ”neutral” context, with the Day Vibration present but not dominant. In contrast, the outer psyche chart shows how the Day Vibration manifests when the personality and ego take up significant space, exerting their influence on the psyche. This is why the outer psyche chart is placed on the outer side of the charts— it represents the ego as it interacts with the world.

Studying the interplay between the ”pure” psyche (inner psyche chart) and the psyche shaped by the ego (outer psyche chart) offers profound insights. It reveals how the ego can inflict pain or, conversely, bring unexpected joy in specific situations. This dynamic is determined by the unique number combinations that emerge when the outer psyche chart is superimposed on the inner one. We’ll explore this fascinating interplay in more detail shortly.

Understanding the Context of the Double Psyche Charts To delve deeper into the double psyche charts, it’s helpful to understand the context in which they were developed and the differing perspectives among numerologists in Scandinavia.

There are four main factions of numerologists, each with distinct approaches: The First Faction: This group focuses solely on the name chart and the Day Vibration, which overlays the name chart. They do not consider higher self numbers or other charts, viewing the name chart as the core of numerology.

The Second Faction: While they include higher self numbers in their interpretations, they completely dismiss the psyche chart. For them, incorporating the month and year into a numeroscope is ”blasphemous.” They firmly believe that a numeroscope is exclusively for analyzing names—end of story.

The Third Faction: This group works with the inner psyche chart, as we just discussed, but rejects the outer psyche chart. They argue that applying the Day Enhancement Rule to anything beyond the name chart is illogical. In fact, many in this faction view the rule itself as clumsy and inelegant but accept it as part of tradition.

The Fourth Faction: This group embraces both the inner and outer psyche charts, despite acknowledging that the Day Enhancement Rule applied in this context may seem inelegant or nonsensical. Their reasoning is simple: the outer psyche chart works. It provides strikingly accurate insights

and generates countless “aha” moments during readings.

#### Our Perspective

We have used the outer psyche chart for years with extraordinary results. Time and again, during group sessions, we witness people amazed by the chart’s uncanny accuracy and its ability to reveal deep truths.

However, this success has always posed a dilemma for us. While we deeply value the insights provided by the outer psyche chart, we find the Day Enhancement Rule frustratingly inelegant. It raises challenging questions—most notably: If this rule is applied to the psyche chart, shouldn’t it also be used in the Vocation or Personality charts? However, applying it in those contexts would create significant complications. Many names when looking only at its vowels or consonants would frequently result in numbers under 10, triggering the Day Enhancement Rule almost constantly. This would not only clutter the charts but also make them far more difficult to interpret and comprehend.

Reconceptualizing the Outer Psyche Chart Without the ”Day Enhancement Rule”

Is there another way to conceptualize the outer psyche chart without relying on the ”Day Enhancement Rule”?

The answer is yes!

Our perspective on the numeroscope chart is that it represents how energies interact under the influence of a primary consciousness principle—almost always the Day Vibration. When we examine the outer psyche chart, we are not simply applying the Day Enhancement Rule. Instead, we are observing the interplay between the year energy (as the inner energy) and the Higher Self Outer Diamond digit sum (as the outer energy). This approach aligns more naturally with the chart’s structure and purpose.

Here’s why this makes more sense:

- The inner psyche chart compares the ”pure” month energy (outer energy) with the year energy (inner energy).

- The outer psyche chart, on the other hand, compares the Outer Diamond digit sum (outer energy) with the year energy (inner energy). This shift in interpretation emphasizes the Outer Diamond’s power in representing outer life and success codes. In fact, the calculation for the Outer Diamond digit sum is identical to what the Day Enhancement Rule would produce: it is the Day Vibration added to the Month Vibration. However, by reframing the explanation, we no longer need to rely on the concept of a ”Day Enhancement Rule.” Instead, we can simply state that:

- A numeroscope can be used to compare any energies.

- The inner psyche chart explores the interplay between the month and year energies.

- The outer psyche chart examines the interplay between the Outer Diamond digit sum and the year energy.

This eliminates the need for the Day Enhancement Rule entirely, resolving several conceptual issues that arose with its use. Further Challenges of the Day Enhancement Rule One of the key problems with the Day Enhancement Rule is its inconsistent application, especially with the year energy. For example:

- In psyche charts, the year is usually represented by its primary digit sum. For instance, 1984 is written as 22/4 in the psyche chart. This approach works well, as the year energy is left intact.

- However, certain years, like 2004, have a digit sum of 6, which is less than 10. According to the Day Enhancement Rule, this would require enhancement. Yet, this happens only for a small subset of years, primarily in the early 2000s, while most other years remain unaffected. This inconsistency feels arbitrary and awkward.

Furthermore, applying the Day Enhancement Rule to the year energy raises deeper questions. The year energy, derived from a large number (e.g., 1984 or 2004), is inherently significant. Reducing it to a digit sum and then modifying it with the Day Vibration diminishes its integrity. Unlike the month energy, where the original value is used in full, the year energy is already a condensed form. Altering it further feels unnecessary and counterintuitive.

#### A More Logical Approach

By leaving the year energy intact, we honor its unique strength. The year energy reflects the flow of consciousness into an incarnation and plays a critical role in determining destiny rows. It is a powerful, standalone energy that does not naturally ”succumb” to the influence of the Day Vibration—even in cases like the year 2004. This revised framework simplifies the conceptual structure of the psyche charts while maintaining their interpretive depth. It removes the need for arbitrary rules and aligns the charts with a more coherent, logical theory, ensuring that each element is respected for its unique contribution.

RECAP: Reframing the Outer and Inner Psyche Charts For these reasons, it makes sense to:

- 1. View the outer and inner psyche charts as comparisons between two powerful constellations. The inner psyche chart compares the month and year energies, while the outer psyche chart compares the Outer Diamond digit sum and the year energy.

- 2. Always preserve the year vibration in its original form. The year vibration is inherently powerful and complete, making any modification, such as applying the Day Enhancement Rule (if such a rule is valid at all), unnecessary and illogical.
