Based on the sources, the fear of death, or "death anxiety," is primarily associated with the karmic number 16/7 and combinations that contain this vibration, as well as the number 13/4. Primary Number for Fear of Death

The number most frequently and strongly associated with the fear of death and existential anxiety is **16/7** (The Tower).

The core lesson of the 16/7 vibration is **transcendence**—learning deeply that **everything in this life is transitory and will die, pass away, and return to nothingness**. This experience can lead to extreme inner turmoil.

In imbalance, 16/7 causes:

* **Existential fear of death** that can create debilitating anxiety.
* Fear of being **annihilated**.
* Feelings of isolation, loneliness, anxiety, and fear.

Specific Combinations Associated with Fear of Death

Several combinations and higher compound numbers are noted for intensifying or containing this fear:

1. **16/7 and 13/4:**
   * When the number **13/4** (associated with death and transformation) is close to **16/7** in the charts, it can cause an **intense fear of death** in the personality.
   * The reversed Tarot card for 13/4 symbolizes the **fear of death** and the fear of change.
2. **11/2 and 13/4 (with 16/7):**
   * The combination of **11/2** next to **13/4** (in the diamond) can lead to **"fear of death"** when the outer world is perceived as dangerous, especially if the outer 7 is formed by **16/7**.
3. **88/7 (The Karma Burner):**
   * This master number has the digit sum **16/7** and brings a fearful nature, specifically being **afraid of the consequences of one's actions**.
   * In imbalance, 88/7 can lead to deep inner fear, paralyzing fear, self-doubt, and hesitation, resulting in a feeling of walking a tightrope ("damned if I do, damned if I don't").
   * Unbalanced 88/7 results in being submerged in a profound existential anxiety.
   * **80/8** (which is engulfed in fear like 88/7) is also mentioned.
4. **79/7:**
   * This number often carries an **existential fear of death** that can create debilitating anxiety.
5. **97/7 (The Transcendentalist):**
   * Before reaching its peak, this number may have to pass through a "valley of **deep existential anxiety and fear**". When mastered, 97/7 has overcome its fear of death.
6. **44/8:**
   * In imbalance, 44/8 includes **anxiety of death** among its difficult aspects.

General Fear and Anxiety Combinations

Other numbers and combinations commonly associated with intense fear and anxiety (which often feed into the fear of death themes):

* **7** (In general): Has a tendency toward fear and existential anxiety.
* **16/7 and 15/6 and 8:** These number combinations can lead to experiencing a lot of **fear and anxiety for seemingly no reason**, and the 16/7 boosts the fear.
* **17/8 (Spotlight Allergy):** Unbalanced 17/8 can develop an **extreme fear of being seen** or heard, leading to paranoia and feeling persecuted, reminiscent of a "witch burning phenomenon". This is also fueled when **11/2** is close to **17/8**.
* **7 and 12/3 and 11/2 (together):** This combination can cause psychological instability, insecurity, and **anxiety**, leading to imaginary dramas.

Fear of Death and Annihilation (Specific Contexts)

The following involve specific placements or combinations that deepen the fear of death, annihilation, or loss of control:

1. **7 and 13/4 Combination:** This pairing results in a subconscious **"death attraction"**.
2. **16/7 + 8 in the Emotion Center:** This combination is described as especially problematic, giving a hard and closed energy and leading to **deep fear of consequences**. The individual constantly walks around with a feeling of guilt and the fear that **"lightning could strike at any moment and you could lose everything you have built up"**.
3. **16/7 + 15/6 in the Mind Center:** This highly difficult combination brings mysterious illness, mental disorders, depression, and **indescribable anxiety**. It suggests excessively open channels, making the person easily influenced by **"evil spirits or low vibrational energies"**.
4. **16/7 in the Dharma Number:** When 16/7 is the Dharma number (the fusion of the conscious and subconscious mind), **fear is identified as the biggest obstacle** preventing the merging of these two aspects.
5. **Unbalanced 70/7 (The Cosmic Seeker):** In imbalance, this number is associated with psychological issues like lacking grounding, feeling burdened by practicality, and being **tense and needing to learn how to relax**. It may also lead to **irrational emotions and self-criticism** and a loss of connection to reality.

Fear Manifesting as Counterphobia or Paranoia

* **Counterphobic Anxiety (16/7 + 9 energy):** A combination of **16/7** and the energy of **9** (including 9, 18/9, or 27/9) can manifest as **counterphobic anxiety**. This means the person indulges in **reckless behavior to purposely go up against their fears**.
* **Fear of the World / Spotlight (17/8 + 16/7):** The combination of **17/8** and **16/7** in the mind center creates a **strong visibility allergy and fear of the world**. It makes sensitive private matters public, leading to great fear and anxiety.

General Anxiety and Instability

The **general 7 vibration** (without specifying 16) is linked to a tendency toward **fear** and **existential anxiety**. It can create a feeling of standing on an **"abyss"**.

The combinations of **7**, **11/2**, and/or **12/3** (especially when placed near the lower right aura, solar plexus, or family name numbers) are widely noted to cause **psychological instability**, **insecurity, and anxiety** in relationships and social life. This can lead the individual to spend too much time overthinking or creating **"imaginary dramas"** in the mind.
