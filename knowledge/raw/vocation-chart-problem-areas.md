There may be a tendency to become hard and brooding and to reap some results that you don't want. There may also be a tendency to observe and control the environment.

Whether the 9 here is in balance or imbalance depends very much on the surrounding numbers and the name chart/vocation chart as a whole.

In balance, the number 9 provides good leadership skills and a great inner drive to succeed and achieve one's goals.

You are in touch with an inner, higher purpose in all that you do.

Statistics show that the number 9 appears most often among the world's top 500 billionaires on the Forbes list in the mind center of the vocation chart.

The number 9 could help influence these people to have a strong drive and desire to reach the top and achieve their goals.
